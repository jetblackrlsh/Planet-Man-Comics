# Treatment

## Logline

In the ruined hush after Bruiser's defeat, one-inch Machine Monarch hijacks Bruiser's hop suit and escapes to a living circuit world, where the world's endless machine voices and Leapona's divine arrogance force him to discover a frightening new scale of power.

## Central Conflict

Machine Monarch wants power large enough to turn survival into conquest. Leapona wants to defend the myth of her divine race and punish a tiny blasphemer who invokes Planet-Man's name. The circuit world itself becomes the third force: alive, wounded by battle, and uniquely vulnerable to Machine Monarch's machine communion.

## Theme

False divinity collapses when someone can command the systems underneath it. Machine Monarch rejects Leapona's godhood, then commits to building his own.

## Emotional Arc

Machine Monarch begins cold and tactical, choosing retreat over revenge. The circuit world's billions of machine voices overwhelm him, briefly turning command into agony. Leapona's attacks make him feel small again, but the exposed wires transform his fear into revelation. By the end, he no longer wants only revenge on Planet-Man; he wants a world to reshape.

## Ending

Machine Monarch electrocutes Leapona with the circuit world's exposed nerves. The falling giant's crash knocks him down, but the event confirms his new relationship with the world. He closes his eyes, listens past the pain, and tells the world, "We have much to build."
