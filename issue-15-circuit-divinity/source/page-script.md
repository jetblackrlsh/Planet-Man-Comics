# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, late-night HQ practical lighting, photoreal white lily pad glyph light, macro-scale circuit-world terrain, realistic wind-blast shockwaves, impact dust, exposed wires, electrical arcs, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Machine Monarch is a one-inch-tall humanoid black robot villain with glossy black and matte gunmetal armor panels, embedded glowing neon green circuit patterns across his whole body, round glowing blue LED eyes, no pupils, no human face, a jagged crown made from green circuit boards and tiny components, and a royal purple cape made from bundled wires and braided cables; he must remain tiny throughout. Bruiser is a massive unconscious muscular adult humanoid frog man with green mottled amphibian skin, black eye patch partly lifted over a cracked ruby cybernetic laser eye, closed unconscious eyes, and a futuristic white leather hop suit with black accents, black armored shoulders, and several slashes. Leapona is a thin adult humanoid frog woman with realistic amphibian skin, thick yellow webbing between fingers and toes, narrow yellow eyes, a severe frog-like face, an afro of thick feathery orange hair, and a futuristic white leather hop suit with black accents; she glides and hovers like a flying giant from Machine Monarch's perspective. Planet-Man is an unconscious battered adult divine humanoid with green vine dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, fully closed unconscious eyes, no eye patch, and a fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a tiny miniature planet. Shrink-Scope is unconscious with a bruised face, polished wooden body with black ruler-like hash marks, fully closed unconscious eyes, black crop top with microscope insignia, black starry miniskirt, and glittery black heels. Hoppette is an unconscious curvy adult humanoid frog woman with fully closed unconscious eyes, no eye patch, long curly brown hair, smooth amphibian skin, and a white-and-black hop suit with a scorch mark on her chest.

Setting continuity: Shrink-Scope HQ main common area late at night, damaged after Issue 14: cracked floor, couch, compact kitchen area, sleeping corridor doors, equipment lockers, repair bench, black surfaces with silver star details, polished wood panels with ruler-like hash marks, crystal lens fixtures, smoke, dust, shattered panels, laser scorch marks, and no readable incidental text. Circuit World is a vast living electronic landscape made from oversized circuit boards, black glass plates, copper traces, glowing green pathways, solder-like hills, upright components, and exposed wire bundles that feel like nerves.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo except intentional cover title text, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting. Do not use speaker-name prefixes.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: announce Machine Monarch's escape from the aftermath into a living circuit world.
Layout: cover image with one strong realistic-photo composition and modest poster clarity, still grounded in candid photo-comic style.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: CIRCUIT DIVINITY". Spell the brand name exactly as "PLANET-MAN" with one E in "PLANET"; never spell it "PLANEET-MAN". Small caption box: "The smallest tyrant found a world that listened."
Visual beats: Tiny Machine Monarch stands on unconscious Bruiser's chest like a conqueror, glowing green circuits echoing the alien circuit world opening behind him through a white lily pad glyph. Bruiser, Planet-Man, Shrink-Scope, and Hoppette are all unconscious with eyes fully closed. Bruiser is the only character with an eye patch; Planet-Man and Hoppette must not have eyepatches. Bruiser's hands must look anatomically normal for a muscular humanoid frog man: proportional to his body, not oversized, not mutated, not swollen, and not distorted by forced perspective. Leapona hovers enormous above the circuit terrain in the background, webbed hands spread, orange feathered afro lit by green circuitry. The damaged HQ floor and unconscious heroes are faintly visible below the glyph light.
