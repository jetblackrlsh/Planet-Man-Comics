# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The pages should feel like documentary phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels, even when the action becomes superpowered. Use believable camera perspective, physical textures, natural imperfect framing, late-night practical HQ lighting, photoreal white lily pad glyph light, realistic circuit-world VFX, violent wind-blast dust, electricity, impact rubble, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays. No speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no speaker-name prefixes, no subtitles, no watermarks, no extra decorative text, and no blank caption boxes.

## Machine Monarch

- Role: shrunken robot villain seeking enough power to rule.
- Scale: only about one inch tall throughout the issue; when beside Bruiser or Leapona, he should look tiny.
- Body: humanoid black robot, glossy black and matte gunmetal armor panels, strong mechanical build.
- Surface motif: glowing neon green circuit pattern across his face, chest, arms, hands, and legs. The circuit lines look embedded under transparent black plating, not painted on.
- Eyes: round glowing blue LED eyes, no pupils, no human face.
- Crown: jagged crown made from green circuit boards, exposed microchips, gold contact pins, and tiny solder points.
- Cape: royal purple cape made from bundled wires and braided cables, with copper and green fiber-optic strands visible along the edges.
- Expression range: cold calculation, pained overload, arrogant defiance, predatory wonder, quiet command.
- Silhouette: upright kingly posture, angular shoulders, crown always visible, wire-cape trailing behind him.
- Issue state: starts standing on Bruiser's unconscious body in the damaged HQ; later blends into the circuit terrain because his green circuit pattern matches the environment; ends dusty and knocked down but spiritually triumphant.

## Bruiser

- Role: unconscious transport anchor and fallen "divine race" warrior.
- Body: massive muscular adult humanoid frog man, much broader and heavier than Hoppette and gigantic from Machine Monarch's perspective, with green mottled amphibian skin, thick neck, powerful frog legs, large frog hands and feet, and stern frog-like face.
- Outfit: futuristic white leather bodysuit with black accents, heavier and more armored than Hoppette's, black armored shoulder pads, black utility seams, no readable labels, no logos.
- Injury state: unconscious for the entire issue; cracked ruby red cybernetic laser eye under lifted black eye patch; hop suit slashed in several places; bruised and scorched from Issue 14.
- Powers and gear: his hop suit can generate glowing white lily pad glyph travel when Machine Monarch whispers through its machinery.

## Leapona

- Role: airborne antagonist from Bruiser and Hoppette's world who believes her race is divine.
- Body: thin adult humanoid frog woman with realistic amphibian skin and long limbs; thick yellow webbing between fingers and toes, large enough to catch air like gliding membranes.
- Face and eyes: narrow yellow eyes, severe frog-like face, mocking smile that hardens into holy rage.
- Hair: afro of thick feathery orange hair, physically textured and voluminous.
- Outfit: futuristic white leather bodysuit with black accents, a sleek hop suit fitted for flight, no readable labels, no logos.
- Powers: glides and hovers through the air using the yellow webbing; sweeps webbed hands to create powerful wind blasts that crater circuit terrain.
- Scale relationship: from one-inch Machine Monarch's perspective, Leapona appears like a giant flying woman looming in the sky.
- Expression range: mocking delight, offended fury, murderous certainty, shocked agony.

## Planet-Man

- Role: unconscious aftermath presence and the forbidden name that enrages Leapona.
- Body: adult divine humanoid normal human scale in Shrink-Scope's world, black rocky volcanic skin with grounded stone texture.
- Face and eyes: molten lava eyes that glow orange when awake, fully closed while unconscious in this issue.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, organic and physically textured.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct tiny miniature planet; no readable labels or logos.
- Issue state: unconscious on the damaged HQ floor, especially battered and bruised after Issue 14; no eye patch.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: unconscious aftermath presence.
- Body: heroic adult woman with polished wooden body covered in black ruler-like hash marks.
- Face and eyes: glowing white crystal-ball eyes when awake, fully closed while unconscious; bruised face.
- Outfit: black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, glittery black heels; no readable labels or logos.
- Issue state: unconscious beside cracked floor where her head hit during Bruiser's attack.

## Hoppette

- Role: unconscious aftermath presence and reminder of the hop-suit world.
- Body: adult humanoid curvy frog woman with smooth realistic amphibian skin, blue eyes, expressive rounded frog-like face, long curly brown hair, frog-like hands and feet.
- Outfit: futuristic flexible white leather bodysuit with black accents, fitted practical panels, subtle travel-device seams, no readable labels, no logos.
- Issue state: unconscious against the back wall with fully closed eyes and a scorch mark on her chest where Bruiser's laser blast hit her; no eye patch.

## Shrink-Scope HQ Main Interior

Late-night common area inside Shrink-Scope's hidden HQ: couch, compact kitchen area, sleeping corridor doors, equipment lockers, repair bench, hidden reinforced door, emergency supplies, black surfaces with silver star details, polished wood panels with ruler-like black hash marks, crystal lens fixtures, microscope-inspired hardware, and no readable incidental text. At the start of Issue 15, the room is badly damaged by Issue 14: cracked floor, shattered wall panels, smoke, dust, laser scorch marks, toppled furniture, and unconscious bodies.

## White Lily Pad Glyph

Photoreal floor magic/technology effect: a glowing white lily pad symbol on the ground, bright enough to cast cold light onto feet, armor, polished surfaces, and rubble. In this issue it is triggered by Bruiser's hop suit after Machine Monarch whispers through the suit's machinery.

## Circuit World

A strange living world where the landscape appears made from oversized circuit boards, embedded copper traces, glassy black plates, glowing green pathways, solder-like hills, upright components, and bundles of exposed wires. It should feel physically present and real, like a photographed macro-electronic terrain scaled into a vast alien landscape. The world is alive or at least inhabited by billions of machine voices, so when Leapona's wind blasts tear open the terrain, the exposed wires should feel like nerves.
