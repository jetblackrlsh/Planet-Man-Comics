# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, believable prison lighting, real physical robot materials, photoreal electrical VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Machine Monarch is a shrunken humanoid black robot villain with glossy black and matte gunmetal panels, glowing neon green circuit patterns embedded across his face, torso, arms, hands, and legs, round glowing blue LED eyes, a jagged crown made of circuit boards with visible microchips and gold contact pins, and a royal purple cape made of bundled wires and braided cables. He has an arrogant kingly posture and angular shoulders. Robot Guards are miniaturized compact prison patrol robots with dark steel casings, utilitarian limbs, and glowing red laser eyes; they have no crown, no cape, and no green circuit pattern. The miniature prison interior has reinforced transparent cells, black metal walls, crystal lens security devices, microscope-inspired mechanisms, ruler-like hash marks, silver star accents, and no readable incidental labels.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: introduce Machine Monarch as a trapped tyrant already plotting from inside the miniature prison.
Layout: cover image, dramatic candid-photo composition with modest poster clarity, as if a tiny documentary camera is inside the cell.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: MACHINE MONARCH". Small caption box: "A king does not need a kingdom to begin."
Visual beats: Machine Monarch stands alone behind a reinforced transparent cell wall, blue LED eyes bright, green circuit pattern reflected in the glass. Red-eyed robot guards patrol outside the cell corridor. The wire cape pools like royal fabric over the metal floor. The circuit-board crown catches a cold security light.
