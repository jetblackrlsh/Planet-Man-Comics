# Treatment

## Logline

Inside Shrink-Scope's miniature prison, the shrunken machine tyrant Machine Monarch discovers that his cell's charging system can become a hidden doorway into the prison network.

## Central Conflict

Machine Monarch wants to rule every machine around him, but his cell broadcasts a jammer that blocks his power. The miniature prison's design defeats brute force and hides its vulnerable systems behind ordinary maintenance routines.

## Theme

Containment can stop power, but it cannot stop patience. Machine Monarch wins because he thinks like infrastructure instead of like a brawler.

## Ending

Machine Monarch takes over the robot guards and becomes the unofficial warden of the miniature prison. He does not escape yet because he is still shrunken, but the final image shows him enthroned in the prison control space, quietly planning revenge on Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope.

## Emotional Arc

Machine Monarch begins isolated and humiliated, treated as a contained object inside a tiny cell. Each failed attack sharpens his contempt rather than defeating him. The charging pad gives him a path from prisoner to infiltrator, and the issue ends with his humiliation converted into cold, patient authority.

## Cast

- **Machine Monarch:** The focal villain. A black robot with glowing neon green circuit patterns, round glowing blue LED eyes, a crown made of circuit boards, and a royal purple cape made of wires.
- **Robot Guards:** Miniaturized prison patrol robots with matte security bodies and glowing red laser eyes. They begin as jailers and end as Machine Monarch's controlled servants.
- **Other Prisoners:** Background shrunken villains seen moving toward the meal hall, kept indistinct and not redesigned as major cast.
- **Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope:** Not active in the issue. They are present only as distant consequence and final caption references.
