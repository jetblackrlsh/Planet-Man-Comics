# Character Bible

## Machine Monarch

- Role: shrunken robot villain imprisoned inside Shrink-Scope's miniature prison.
- Body: humanoid black robot, glossy black and matte gunmetal panels, strong mechanical build, visibly smaller than normal because he is inside a miniature prison.
- Surface motif: glowing neon green circuit pattern across his face, chest, arms, hands, and legs. The circuit lines should look embedded under transparent black plating, not painted on.
- Eyes: round glowing blue LED eyes, no pupils, no human face.
- Crown: jagged crown made of green circuit boards, exposed microchips, gold contact pins, and tiny solder points.
- Cape: royal purple cape made from bundled wires and braided cables, with copper and green fiber-optic strands visible along the edges.
- Expression range: arrogant stillness, cold calculation, frustrated restraint, triumphant command. His face remains robotic, so emotion is shown through posture, eye brightness, hand gestures, cape movement, and lighting.
- Silhouette: upright kingly posture, angular shoulders, crown always visible, wire-cape trailing behind him.

## Robot Guards

- Role: miniaturized robotic prison patrol guards.
- Body: compact armored service robots with reinforced black and dark steel casings, utilitarian limbs, and small baton-like tools.
- Eyes: glowing red laser eyes, usually two narrow red lenses.
- Motion: patrol in disciplined formation until Machine Monarch controls them; then they kneel, unlock doors, and stand like royal guards.
- Keep them visually distinct from Machine Monarch: no crown, no cape, no green circuit pattern.

## Miniature Prison Interior

- Scale: a tiny high-security containment facility inside Shrink-Scope's HQ, but the pages are shot from inside the miniature prison so it feels like a real prison environment at tiny scale.
- Visual language: reinforced transparent cell fronts, black metal walls, crystal lens security emitters, microscope-inspired mechanisms, ruler-like hash marks, silver star accents, tiny warning lights with no readable labels.
- Machine Monarch's cell: clean reinforced box with hidden seams, smooth wall panels, no exposed devices, faint green and blue reflections from his body.
- Charging pad: appears when a wall panel flips around; a flat illuminated induction plate with subtle blue-white electrical glow and hidden contact rings.
- Control system: abstract glowing circuitry, small diagnostic panels, and crystal-lens jammer core; no legible incidental text.

## Continuity Notes

- Machine Monarch remains shrunken throughout. Do not show him at full size in this issue.
- The jammer is invisible or hidden until Machine Monarch accesses it through the charging pad.
- Use caption boxes only for all narration and rare short speech. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating subtitles, watermarks, logos, or separate text overlays.
- All readable story text must be generated directly inside the comic page art.
