# Treatment

## Title

Planet-Man: Nothing Much

## Logline

While Planet-Man is away on a mission, Shrink-Scope handles a bank robbery
alone and proves that her wooden body and clairvoyance make her dangerous even
without using size alteration.

## Central Conflict

Shrink-Scope chooses whether to call Planet-Man for a seemingly small threat or
trust her own judgment and powers. The robber appears ordinary at first, but
his super strength turns the encounter into a real test.

## Theme

Scale is not the same as importance. Shrink-Scope does not need Planet-Man's
cosmic force to protect people; she has her own disciplined, grounded kind of
strength.

## Ending

Shrink-Scope defeats the super-strong robber, quietly hands him to the police,
and returns to HQ. When Planet-Man asks what she did, she underplays the whole
fight as "Nothing much."

## Emotional Arc

Shrink-Scope begins in command mode, makes an independent choice, absorbs the
first impact without fear, adapts when the robber reveals he is superpowered,
and ends with relaxed confidence rather than triumphal boasting.

## Page Count

Eight pages: cover plus seven story pages.
