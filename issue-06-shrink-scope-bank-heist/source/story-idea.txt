Issue 6 explores more of what Shrink-Scope can do while Planet-Man is already
on another mission.

Shrink-Scope has a sudden clairvoyant vision of a gunman robbing a bank. She
considers contacting Planet-Man, but decides this is too small-scale for him
and that she can handle it herself. She has already used her one daily
size-alteration ability on a villain they defeated earlier, so she must rely
only on clairvoyance, focus, and her wooden body.

The robber runs out of the bank with a bag of money, looking back with a smug
grin. He does not see Shrink-Scope standing heroically in his path. He slams
into her at full force and bounces backward as if he hit a tree. She does not
budge.

The robber threatens her, opens fire, and Shrink-Scope uses breathwork to
compress the elastic bark of her arms, catching the bullets before they can
penetrate. The robber reveals super strength by cracking the pavement with a
stomp, then charges her.

Shrink-Scope's crystal-ball eyes shift colors as she catches glimpses of the
near future. She dodges his overhead strike, kick, right hook, and tackle, then
counters with an uppercut carrying the force of a falling sequoia. The blow
launches him through the clouds. She waits impatiently for him to fall, catches
his unconscious body, and hands him to the police.

Back at HQ, Planet-Man asks what she has been up to today. Shrink-Scope replies
casually, "Nothing much."
