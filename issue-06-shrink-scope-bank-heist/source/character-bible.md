# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The pages should feel like documentary
phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels, even when
the action becomes superpowered. Use believable camera perspective, physical
textures, natural imperfect framing, photoreal VFX, realistic street lighting,
and clean rectangular caption boxes. No anime, no painted illustration, no
cartoon rendering, no comic-book linework, no studio-poster polish except on
the cover.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or
cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: protagonist and field hero for this issue.
- Body: athletic heroic woman with polished wooden body, visible grain, and
  black ruler-like hash marks across her face, arms, torso, and legs.
- Face: calm focused expression range, raised eyebrow when unimpressed, glowing
  white crystal-ball eyes that can shift into faint prismatic color during
  clairvoyant near-future glimpses.
- Hair: warm medium-brown hair pulled back in a practical tied-back bun.
- Outfit: black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt
  covered in silver stars, glittery black heels.
- Powers used here: clairvoyance, near-future combat timing, wooden body,
  compressible elastic bark skin, and strength carrying the might of ancient
  trees. She does not use size alteration in this issue.
- Motifs: crystal optics, microscope shapes, ruler marks, silver stars, black
  practical superhero gear, polished bark texture, calm heroic posture.

## Planet-Man

- Role: supporting character, seen only on mission in the opening and at HQ in
  the final page.
- Body: divine humanoid with black rocky volcanic skin, rough stone texture,
  subtle molten fissures, and glowing molten lava eyes.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, organic and root-like, around his face and
  shoulders.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit; every bead is a distinct tiny miniature
  planet with oceans, clouds, rock, ice, fire, rings, forests, metal, or alien
  terrain.
- Personality here: busy on a larger mission, then casually curious when
  Shrink-Scope returns.

## Bank Robber

- Role: antagonist, a super-strong robber pretending to be just a common
  gunman.
- Body: stocky adult man, broad shoulders, rough stubble, flushed face,
  bruiser build.
- Outfit: dark red hooded jacket under a worn black leather coat, dark jeans,
  black boots, cheap black gloves, half-mask or low scarf around the lower
  face that slips during the fight.
- Props: handgun, overstuffed cloth money sack with no readable text, scattered
  loose bills.
- Powers: super strength, pavement-cracking stomp, heavy strikes, tackle.
- Emotional range: smug, threatening, shocked, angry, overconfident, then
  unconscious.

## Key Locations

- Shrink-Scope HQ Mission Control Room: functional superhero operations room
  with central planning table, abstract city map screens with no readable
  text, crystal lens viewing apparatus, microscope-inspired consoles, black
  surfaces with silver star accents, polished wood trim with ruler-like black
  hash marks, glowing crystal optics, and practical organization.
- City Bank Exterior: dense urban street outside a stone bank, damaged curb,
  glass doors, alarms and police lights implied, no readable signage, no
  legible brand names.
- City Street Fight Area: pavement in front of the bank, dropped money sack,
  cracked concrete, streetlights, emergency vehicles arriving near the end,
  bystanders kept safely distant.
