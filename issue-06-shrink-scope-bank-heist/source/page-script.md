# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a
complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted
illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism
panels with natural imperfect framing, believable lighting, real physical
materials, photoreal action VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Shrink-Scope is an athletic heroic woman with warm
medium-brown hair in a practical tied-back bun, glowing white crystal-ball eyes,
a polished wooden body with visible grain and black ruler-like hash marks, a
black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, a black miniskirt covered in
silver stars, and glittery black heels. Planet-Man is a divine humanoid with
green vine dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten lava eyes, and a fitted
beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct miniature planet. The bank
robber is a stocky adult man with broad shoulders, rough stubble, a dark red
hooded jacket under a worn black leather coat, dark jeans, black boots, cheap
black gloves, a slipping lower-face scarf, a handgun, and an overstuffed cloth
money sack with no readable text.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no
floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable
decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must
be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or
art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: announce a Shrink-Scope solo issue and the bank-robbery conflict.
Layout: cover image, dramatic candid-photo composition with modest poster
clarity. Shrink-Scope stands calmly in the middle of a city street outside a
bank while the robber recoils backward from colliding with her wooden body;
loose money flies, the dropped sack tumbles, and faint prismatic reflections
glow in her crystal eyes.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: NOTHING
MUCH". Small caption box: "Shrink-Scope handles it."

## Page 02 The Vision

Story role: establish Planet-Man is away, Shrink-Scope sees the bank robbery,
and she chooses to handle it alone.
Layout: six documentary panels in HQ and city-vision fragments.
Caption boxes:
1. "Planet-Man was already on a mission."
2. "Shrink-Scope saw another crisis bloom."
3. "A gunman. A bank. Seconds away."
4. "She almost called him."
5. "Her size-change was already spent."
6. "This one was hers."
Visual beats: Planet-Man on a distant mission screen, surrounded by miniature
planets in action; Shrink-Scope at crystal lens controls in HQ; her eyes
filling with a fractured image of a bank robber; close-up of her hand hovering
near comms controls; her looking at a small containment display implying an
earlier defeated villain; Shrink-Scope leaving HQ with calm focus.

## Page 03 Like A Tree

Story role: show the robber escaping and failing to move Shrink-Scope.
Layout: six street-level action panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "The robber ran out laughing."
2. "He looked back, not forward."
3. "Shrink-Scope was already there."
4. "He hit her at full speed."
5. "Wood does not yield easily."
6. "The money hit the ground first."
Visual beats: robber bursting from bank doors with money sack; close-up of his
smug grin as he looks back; low-angle reveal of Shrink-Scope standing in his
path; full-body collision with him crumpling against her torso while she does
not budge; impact shock with bark texture visible; robber thrown backward,
money sack dropping and bills scattering.

## Page 04 Bark And Bullets

Story role: show Shrink-Scope's wooden body and breathwork defense under fire.
Layout: six panels, tense but controlled.
Caption boxes:
1. Robber short speech: "Scram. This is my heist!"
2. Shrink-Scope short speech: "I'm not here for the money."
3. Shrink-Scope short speech: "I'm here to stop you."
4. Robber short speech: "I'd like to see you try."
5. "She crossed her arms and breathed."
6. "Elastic bark caught every bullet."
Visual beats: robber scrambling up and shouting with handgun visible; close-up
of Shrink-Scope raising one eyebrow; Shrink-Scope calm in the street as money
flutters around her; robber pointing the gun; gunfire streaking toward her
crossed wooden forearms; compressed bark dimples around bullets stopped in her
arms without blood.

## Page 05 Future Colors

Story role: reveal the robber is superpowered and Shrink-Scope's clairvoyance
becomes combat timing.
Layout: six kinetic panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "The robber stopped smiling."
2. Robber short speech: "Bet you can't fight."
3. "His stomp cracked the pavement."
4. Shrink-Scope short speech: "You're a super?"
5. "Her crystal eyes changed colors."
6. "The next second arrived early."
Visual beats: robber shocked by the caught bullets; him snarling and rolling
his shoulders; his boot stomping down and spiderweb cracks spreading through
concrete; Shrink-Scope surprised but steady; her glowing crystal eyes shifting
through prismatic colors; ghostly near-future overlays of the robber's first
attack around her.

## Page 06 The Weave

Story role: show Shrink-Scope dodging the robber's attacks with clairvoyant
precision.
Layout: seven narrow action panels, fast reading rhythm.
Caption boxes:
1. "The overhead strike missed."
2. "The kick found empty air."
3. "The hook passed her cheek."
4. "Every glimpse gave her a beat."
5. "He lunged for the tackle."
6. "She planted her roots."
7. "Then the forest answered."
Visual beats: robber bringing a heavy fist down as Shrink-Scope steps aside;
robber sweeping a kick while she pivots; right hook missing by inches with her
crystal eyes glowing; montage of colored future afterimages; robber launching
into a full-body tackle; Shrink-Scope lowering her stance with wooden legs
rooted into cracked pavement; her fist rising with bark grain and leaflike
energy gathering around it.

## Page 07 Sequoia Uppercut

Story role: resolve the fight with Shrink-Scope's decisive strength and calm
comedy.
Layout: six panels, vertical action and timing gag.
Caption boxes:
1. "A normal punch would not do."
2. "She carried older strength."
3. "A falling sequoia has weight."
4. "The clouds swallowed him."
5. "It took a moment."
6. "She caught him on the way down."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope's uppercut landing under the robber's jaw with
photoreal shockwave and bark texture; the robber launched upward between tall
buildings; tiny silhouette punching through low clouds; Shrink-Scope standing
alone below, tapping her foot impatiently; robber falling back down unconscious
from the sky; Shrink-Scope extending one wooden arm and catching him safely
across it.

## Page 08 Nothing Much

Story role: hand off the robber and close with Shrink-Scope's understated
confidence at HQ.
Layout: six panels, street aftermath into HQ.
Caption boxes:
1. "The police arrived after the hard part."
2. "Shrink-Scope handed over the evidence."
3. "No size-change. No Planet-Man."
4. "Back at HQ, the big mission was over too."
5. Planet-Man short speech: "What were you up to today?"
6. Shrink-Scope short speech: "Nothing much."
Visual beats: police cars arriving with lights but no readable markings;
Shrink-Scope calmly handing unconscious robber to police beside the money sack;
close-up of bullets lodged harmlessly in her compressed bark forearms as they
fall out; exterior transition back to hidden HQ; Planet-Man entering mission
control with minor dust and hovering mini-planets, asking casually; final panel
of Shrink-Scope at the planning table, relaxed raised eyebrow, crystal eyes
soft, giving the understated reply.
