# Treatment

## Logline

After a tiring mission, Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope find Hoppette bursting into their hidden HQ through a glowing lily pad glyph, forcing them to stop an agile intruder before learning she came to ask why Planet-Man helps only one of his many worlds.

## Central Conflict

Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope treat Hoppette as a threat because she appears inside their headquarters without warning. Hoppette is too fast and too agile to catch by simple force, but Shrink-Scope's clairvoyance and Planet-Man's miniature gravity trap turn the fight into a conversation about the worlds Planet-Man has left waiting.

## Theme

Responsibility grows when a creator realizes every world still matters, but care has to become a system instead of a single exhausted hero trying to be everywhere.

## Ending

Shrink-Scope proposes a way forward: miniature-planet golems that can carry Planet-Man's help beyond one world. Planet-Man begins making one immediately, and Hoppette leaves through her lily pad glyph carrying the first golem back to another world.

## Emotional Arc

Planet-Man begins depleted and defensive, pulled between the comfort of rest and the shock of an intruder. Hoppette's question exposes a larger moral pressure he cannot dismiss. Shrink-Scope turns that pressure into a practical invention, letting Planet-Man move from guilt to focused creation while Hoppette leaves with hope instead of resentment.

## Cast

- **Planet-Man:** Creator god hero at normal usable scale in Shrink-Scope's world. He is tired after a mission but still protective of the HQ and willing to face the implications of helping many worlds.
- **Shrink-Scope:** Strategic hero and mission controller. She uses clairvoyance to predict Hoppette's leaps and proposes the golem solution after recognizing the same bottleneck in saving her world.
- **Hoppette:** New visitor from another miniature planet. A humanoid curvy frog woman with blue eyes, long curly brown hair, and a futuristic leather white bodysuit with black accents. She moves by superhuman hopping, sticks to walls with mucus, and travels between miniature-planets through glowing white lily pad glyphs.
- **Miniature-Planet Golem:** A first prototype helper body made from small orbiting planets and planetoids, built so Planet-Man can send aid to worlds where he cannot personally appear.
