# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, believable HQ lighting, grounded physical materials, photoreal cosmic and portal VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is an adult humanoid creator god at normal human scale, with green vine dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten lava eyes, and a fitted bodysuit made from many distinct tiny miniature planets as beads. Shrink-Scope is a heroic adult woman with polished wood skin covered in black ruler-like hash marks, long brown hair pulled back behind her head in a practical tied-back style matching pages 3-8, glowing white crystal ball eyes that flare with many colors during clairvoyance, a black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, a black miniskirt with silver stars, and glittery black heels; Shrink-Scope is never bald. Hoppette is an adult humanoid curvy frog woman with blue eyes, long curly brown hair, smooth realistic amphibian skin details, frog-like hands and feet, and a futuristic flexible leather white bodysuit with black accents and subtle travel-device seams. Shrink-Scope's HQ interior is a compact functional superhero base with black surfaces, silver stars, polished wood panels with ruler-like marks, crystal lens fixtures, microscope-inspired hardware, equipment lockers, couch, repair bench, and no readable incidental labels.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: present Hoppette's sudden arrival as a dynamic HQ intrusion and signal the issue's question about worlds beyond this one.
Layout: cover image with dramatic candid-photo clarity, one large HQ interior composition.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: HOPPETTE". Small caption box: "Every world wants its turn."
Visual beats: Hoppette springs upward from a glowing white lily pad glyph on the HQ floor, long curly brown hair flying, blue eyes wide, white-and-black bodysuit catching the portal light. Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope stand in the doorway dusty and exhausted, immediately turning defensive. Miniature planets hover near Planet-Man's hands, and Shrink-Scope's crystal eyes begin to glow with colored clairvoyant light.

## Page 02 The Door In The Floor

Story role: establish the tired return, the HQ, and Hoppette's impossible entrance.
Layout: six documentary panels in reading order.
Caption boxes:
1. "The mission ended late."
2. "Planet-Man wanted rest."
3. "Shrink-Scope wanted quiet."
4. "The floor answered first."
5. "A stranger hopped through light."
6. Hoppette short speech: "Wait, please."
Visual beats: exterior-to-interior return or doorway shot of Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope entering HQ tired and dusty; their shoulders drop in relief; close-up of a glowing white lily pad glyph forming on the floor; Hoppette emerging mid-hop; Planet-Man raising orbiting mini-planets; Shrink-Scope stepping into a guarded stance.

## Page 03 Future Paths

Story role: show Shrink-Scope using clairvoyance while Planet-Man rushes Hoppette.
Layout: five panels with split attention between prediction and action.
Caption boxes:
1. "They did not know her."
2. "So they treated her like danger."
3. "Planet-Man moved first."
4. "Shrink-Scope looked ahead."
5. "The future came in colors."
Visual beats: Planet-Man launching forward in a low flying tackle across the HQ; Hoppette crouching to leap away; Shrink-Scope's crystal eyes glowing with rainbow refractions; ghostly translucent future trails show Hoppette's possible hops across walls and ceiling; final panel shows Shrink-Scope calmly tracking the one path that matters.

## Page 04 Too Fast To Catch

Story role: demonstrate Hoppette's agility and wall-sticking mucus, and show Planet-Man cannot catch her by flying straight at her.
Layout: six fast candid action panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "Hoppette moved like a thrown spring."
2. "Planet-Man had power."
3. "She had angles."
4. "He missed once."
5. "She stuck where feet should fail."
6. "He missed again."
Visual beats: Hoppette vaulting over a couch; Planet-Man streaking through the space she just left; glistening clear mucus handprints and footprints on a wall; Hoppette stuck sideways to the wall in a crouch; Planet-Man correcting in midair; Hoppette kicking off the wall before he reaches her.

## Page 05 The Waiting Fist

Story role: Shrink-Scope turns the chase by standing exactly where Hoppette must land.
Layout: six panels, tactical rhythm and sudden impact.
Caption boxes:
1. "Shrink-Scope stopped chasing."
2. "She already knew the next hop."
3. "Planet-Man missed on purpose or by luck."
4. "Hoppette chose the open path."
5. "It was not open."
6. "The fight hit the floor."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope watching colored future trails; Planet-Man flying past Hoppette again; Hoppette twisting away with confident athletic motion; Shrink-Scope stepping into the exact landing line; Hoppette leaping directly into Shrink-Scope's fist; Hoppette knocked down onto the HQ floor, surprised but not badly hurt.

## Page 06 No Place To Leap

Caption boxes:
1. "Planet-Man did not give her a wall."
2. "He gave her an orbit."
3. "Gravity took hold."
4. "Without a surface, Hoppette had no hop."
5. Hoppette short speech: "I'm not your enemy."
6. Shrink-Scope short speech: "Then how did you find us?"
Visual beats: Planet-Man summoning a dense miniature planet with heavy localized gravitational pull; Hoppette lifting from the floor; small debris and loose tools orbiting; Hoppette rotating helplessly around the mini-planet with limbs tucked; Shrink-Scope standing beneath with guarded focus; Hoppette pleading while suspended in the air.

2. "She had followed the maker's trace."
3. Hoppette short speech: "Help more than one."
4. "Planet-Man heard the weight of it."
5. Planet-Man short speech: "This world still needs me."

Visual beats: Hoppette lowered from the gravity trap but still watched carefully; close-up of white-and-black suit seams glowing like lily pad circuitry; small holographic or VFX hints of many miniature planets; Planet-Man looking troubled, mini-planet beads visible across his suit; Shrink-Scope nodding beside him, serious; Hoppette pouting with sincere frustration, not villainous.

## Page 08 The First Golem

Story role: resolve the debate with Shrink-Scope's golem idea and Hoppette carrying hope back through the glyph.
Layout: seven panels with a visual build sequence and final departure.
Caption boxes:
1. "Planet-Man could not be everywhere."
2. "Shrink-Scope had been counting that problem too."
3. Shrink-Scope short speech: "Could your planets become helpers?"
4. "The idea took shape at once."
5. "Worlds became limbs."
6. Hoppette short speech: "Thank you."
7. "One more world would not wait alone."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope gesturing to Planet-Man's orbiting mini-planets as a practical plan forms; Planet-Man smiling with renewed focus; miniature planets assembling into a small humanoid golem with orbital trails; Hoppette smiling and carefully lifting the golem; the glowing white lily pad glyph reopening on the floor; final panel shows Hoppette hopping into the glyph carrying the miniature-planet golem while Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope watch, tired but hopeful.
