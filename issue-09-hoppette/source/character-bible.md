# Character Bible

## Planet-Man

- Role: creator god hero, returning from a mission and later building the first miniature-planet golem.
- Body: adult humanoid figure, normal human scale inside Shrink-Scope's world, black rocky volcanic skin with grounded stone texture.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, slightly dusty and loose from the prior mission.
- Eyes: molten lava eyes with orange glow, expressive through heat shimmer and brow posture.
- Outfit: fitted bodysuit made of many distinct tiny miniature planets as beads, each bead a tiny world with varied terrain, clouds, oceans, lava, or cities.
- Powers: flight, telekinetic mini-planet control, localized gravity fields, and construction of planet-based tools.
- Expression range: exhausted relief, protective alarm, focused combat, troubled reflection, creative determination.
- Silhouette: tall grounded superhero posture, green vine hair, black volcanic body, orbiting miniature planets.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: tactical hero, clairvoyant, and the person who turns Hoppette's accusation into a workable plan.
- Body: heroic adult woman with polished wood skin covered in black ruler-like hash marks.
- Hair: long brown hair pulled back behind her head in a practical tied-back style, matching her appearance on pages 3-8. She is never bald.
- Eyes: usually glowing white crystal ball eyes; during clairvoyance they flare with many colors like refracted crystal, showing future paths.
- Outfit: black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt with silver stars, glittery black heels.
- Powers: clairvoyance and one size-change per day, though this issue emphasizes clairvoyant prediction.
- Expression range: tired discipline, immediate suspicion, precise tactical focus, guarded empathy, practical excitement.
- Silhouette: compact alert stance, crystal eyes, ruler-marked wood body, starry black outfit.

## Hoppette

- Role: apparent intruder who becomes a messenger from Planet-Man's other neglected worlds.
- Body: adult humanoid curvy frog woman, athletic and agile, with smooth realistic amphibian skin details and frog-like hands and feet.
- Face and eyes: blue eyes, expressive rounded face with subtle frog features, not monstrous.
- Hair: long curly brown hair, moving wildly during leaps.
- Outfit: futuristic leather white bodysuit with black accents, fitted but practical, flexible panels, subtle travel-device seams, no readable labels.
- Powers: superhuman leaping, wall-sticking mucus, and travel between miniature-planets through glowing white lily pad glyphs produced by her suit.
- Expression range: startled defensiveness, agile confidence, pouty frustration, sincere appeal, grateful hope.
- Silhouette: spring-loaded crouch, long curls, white-and-black bodysuit, frog-like hands and feet, luminous lily pad glyph motif.

## Shrink-Scope HQ Interior

- Location: main common area and mission-control-adjacent interior of Shrink-Scope's hidden HQ.
- Visual language: compact functional superhero base, dark black surfaces, silver star details, polished wood panels with ruler-like hash marks, crystal lens fixtures, microscope-inspired hardware, repair bench, couch, equipment lockers, hidden reinforced doors.
- Continuity detail: the white lily pad glyph appears on the HQ floor as a glowing portal-like mark, then reappears at the end.

## Miniature-Planet Golem

- Role: prototype helper created from Planet-Man's mini-planets.
- Body: humanoid golem made of many small realistic miniature planets and planetoids connected by controlled orbital gravity, with tiny clouds, oceans, lava, forests, cities, and rocky pieces forming head, torso, arms, and legs.
- Motion: assembled by Planet-Man's telekinesis, held together by visible orbital trails and localized gravity.
- Scale: small enough for Hoppette to carry, but clearly alive or functional as a mission helper.

## Continuity Notes

- Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope look tired at the start but not injured beyond dust and scuffs.
- Hoppette remains the same design throughout: blue eyes, long curly brown hair, futuristic white leather bodysuit with black accents, frog-woman features, agile posture.
- The glowing white lily pad glyph is Hoppette's travel signature and should appear on page 2 and page 8.
- Use caption boxes only for all narration and rare short speech. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating subtitles, watermarks, logos, or separate text overlays.
- All readable story text must be generated directly inside the comic page art.
