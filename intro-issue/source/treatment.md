# Planet-Man: First Orbit

## Logline

A restless creator god who forgets the worlds he makes is pulled into one of
those worlds by its last true hero, and his first mission teaches him that a
creation is not finished just because he looked away.

## Central Conflict

Planet-Man wants freedom, novelty, and the thrill of making the next impossible
world. Shrink-Scope wants him to take responsibility for the endangered planet
he made. Their first mission forces him to decide whether his powers are only
for creation or also for care.

## Theme

Creation without attention becomes abandonment. Imagination matters most when it
protects what already exists.

## Main Cast

Planet-Man: A divine humanoid creator with green vine dreadlocks, black rocky
volcanic skin, molten lava eyes, and a fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead
is a different miniature planet. He uses Mini-Planetkinesis to summon, shape,
and telekinetically control miniature worlds from marble-size to boulder-size.
His combat style, Planet-Fu, uses mini-planets as drones, shields, bludgeons,
constructs, traps, and explosive or elemental projectiles. He is artistic,
playful, impatient, distractible, and uncomfortable with boredom.

Shrink-Scope: A disciplined heroic woman with glowing white crystal-ball eyes
and a polished wooden body covered in black ruler-like hash marks. She wears a
black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, a black miniskirt with silver
stars, and glittery black heels. She has clairvoyance and can change the size of
one thing per day. She is serious, focused, morally intense, practical, and
desperate to save her villain-filled world.

The Problem World: Shrink-Scope's home planet, a dense urban world with many
superpowered people and very few heroes. Villains exploit the city because there
are too many threats for Shrink-Scope to handle alone.

Lord Radius: A one-issue obstacle, not the franchise center. He is a city-level
villain in a heavy circular armored coat with lens-like shoulder rings and a
glowing expanding force halo. He is trying to enlarge a destructive radius field
through a city block. He exists to demonstrate the kind of threat future issues
will feature.

## Emotional Arc

Planet-Man begins as a fascinated maker who treats worlds like unfinished
sketches. Shrink-Scope confronts him with the cost of that neglect. After being
shrunk into her world, he first treats the mission like a strange new game, then
gets distracted by the beauty of the city and its powers. When civilians are
endangered, he chooses focus. The ending shows him accepting a room in
Shrink-Scope's HQ, not as a prisoner, but as a creator learning to stay.

## Ending

Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope stop Lord Radius. Shrink-Scope shrinks the captured
villain into her miniature prison. Planet-Man looks at the saved city and then
at the unfinished worlds stored in the HQ. He accepts that every future issue
will be a mission: locate a threat, focus the maker, use planets creatively,
save part of the world, and learn what responsibility demands.
