# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The images should feel like documentary
phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels. Natural
lighting, believable physical textures, imperfect framing, realistic locations,
and photoreal power effects. No anime, no painted illustration, no glossy
superhero poster polish except modestly on the cover.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or
cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays.

## Planet-Man

- Role: protagonist, creator god, reluctant field operative.
- Body: divine humanoid with black rocky volcanic skin, rough stone texture, and
  subtle molten fissures.
- Face: angular stone face, molten lava eyes glowing orange, intense but often
  curious expression.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, organic and root-like, hanging around his face
  and shoulders.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit; every bead is a distinct tiny miniature
  planet with oceans, clouds, rock, ice, fire, rings, or forests.
- Powers: Mini-Planetkinesis. He summons and telekinetically controls miniature
  planets; uses them to fly by lifting his own planet-bead suit; uses Planet-Fu
  in combat with orbiting planets, shields, constructs, bludgeons, explosive
  unstable cores, volcanic planets, ice planets, gas planets, water planets, and
  ringed bladed planets.
- Personality: imaginative, artistic, restless, easily distracted, novelty
  seeking, not malicious, sometimes irresponsible, capable of wonder and growth.
- Motif: orbiting marble-to-boulder-size mini-planets, molten orange glow, green
  vine shapes, real-world dust and debris reacting to gravity-like force.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: co-lead, mission controller, hero of the endangered world.
- Body: heroic woman with polished wooden body, black ruler-like hash marks
  across skin and limbs.
- Face: focused face with glowing white crystal-ball eyes, serious expression.
- Outfit: black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt
  covered in silver stars, glittery black heels.
- Powers: clairvoyance and the ability to change the size of one thing per day.
- Personality: disciplined, direct, brave, morally serious, pragmatic, deeply
  protective of her world, willing to make hard choices.
- Motif: crystal optics, microscope shapes, ruler marks, silver stars, black
  practical superhero gear, miniature prison.

## Lord Radius

- Role: sample mission villain for the introductory issue.
- Body: adult supervillain, human-scale, physically real, threatening presence.
- Outfit: heavy circular armored coat with battered dark metal plates, lens-like
  ring structures at shoulders and wrists, glowing expanding force halo around
  torso.
- Power: projects expanding circular pressure fields that can crush streets and
  push buildings apart.
- Personality: arrogant, predatory, enjoys turning public space into his own
  territory.
- Motif: concentric rings, cracked pavement, circular shockwaves, amber warning
  light.

## Key Locations

- Planet-Man's world-storage/workshop: human-scale room with shelves,
  containment rings, inspection lenses, unfinished miniature planets, volcanic
  black materials, green vine-like cables, molten orange accent lights.
- Shrink-Scope's world: dense threatened city with damaged streets, emergency
  lighting, powered figures in the distance, improvised barricades, and ordinary
  civilians caught between villains.
- Shrink-Scope HQ: hidden optical repair/storage building; inside are mission
  control, living quarters, miniature prison storage, crystal lens apparatus,
  black surfaces with silver stars, polished wood trim with ruler marks.
