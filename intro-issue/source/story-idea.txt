Planet-Man introductory issue.

Create a realistic candid photo-comic that introduces Planet-Man, Shrink-Scope,
their powers, their motivations, their personalities, their mission, and the
basic structure of future Planet-Man stories.

Core series pattern:
- Planet-Man is a creator god with Mini-Planetkinesis. He creates and controls
  miniature planets, using them as art, tools, weapons, drones, and protective
  constructs.
- Planet-Man is imaginative, restless, easily distracted, and driven by novelty.
  He is not cruel, but he forgets creations once a newer idea captures him.
- Shrink-Scope is a heroic woman from one of his worlds. She has clairvoyance
  and can change the size of one thing per day.
- Her world is overrun by superpowered villains. She shrinks Planet-Man down to
  normal usable scale for her world so he can help stop them.
- Shrink-Scope locates threats, keeps Planet-Man focused, and stores shrunken
  villains in a miniature prison.
- Future stories should feel like serious, strange missions: Shrink-Scope finds
  a villain, Planet-Man gets fascinated or distracted, the mission tests his
  responsibility as a creator, and the team saves a piece of the world.
