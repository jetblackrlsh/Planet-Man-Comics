# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a
complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted
illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism
panels with natural imperfect framing, believable lighting, real physical
materials, photoreal action VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is a divine humanoid with green vine
dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten lava eyes, and a fitted beaded
bodysuit where every bead is a distinct miniature planet. Shrink-Scope is a
heroic woman with glowing white crystal-ball eyes, a polished wooden body with
black ruler-like hash marks, a black crop top with a crystal microscope
insignia, a black miniskirt with silver stars, and glittery black heels. Lord
Radius is a city villain in a heavy circular armored coat with lens-like rings
and an expanding force halo.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no
floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable
decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must
be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or
art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: signal the series identity and the core team dynamic.
Layout: cover image, one dramatic candid-photo composition with slight poster
clarity. Planet-Man hovers above a damaged city street with miniature planets
orbiting from his bodysuit; Shrink-Scope stands on a rooftop edge in the
foreground, crystal eyes glowing, pointing him toward trouble below.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: FIRST
ORBIT". Small caption box: "A creator god. A desperate hero. One world out of
time."

## Page 02 The Maker

Story role: explain Planet-Man's motivation, personality, and powers.
Layout: four documentary panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "Planet-Man could make worlds."
2. "He loved the first spark of a new idea."
3. "Then the timer in his mind ran out."
4. "Every bead on his suit became another forgotten planet."
Visual beats: Planet-Man shaping a marble-size ocean world; boulder-size
volcanic world hovering near shelves; distracted close-up of molten eyes
looking toward a new idea; final panel shows his beaded bodysuit full of tiny
living worlds.

## Page 03 The Hero Who Stayed

Story role: introduce Shrink-Scope, her world, her motivation, and her limits.
Layout: five panels, rooftop to street to miniature prison.
Caption boxes:
1. "Shrink-Scope lived on one of his forgotten worlds."
2. "Her planet had powers everywhere and mercy almost nowhere."
3. "Clairvoyance showed her the next disaster."
4. "Once a day, she could change one thing's size."
5. "One choice had to matter."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope overlooking a damaged city; villains in distant
streets; crystal eyes reflecting a threat; her hand over a glowing size-change
field; miniature prison on a secured tabletop.

## Page 04 The Hard Choice

Story role: explain why Shrink-Scope shrinks Planet-Man and starts the mission
structure.
Layout: six panels, mission-control sequence.
Caption boxes:
1. "She found the maker through the future."
2. "She did not ask for a miracle."
3. "She used her one change."
4. "Planet-Man fell into the scale of her world."
5. Planet-Man short speech in caption box: "You shrank a god?"
6. Shrink-Scope short speech in caption box: "I recruited one."
Visual beats: crystal lens apparatus locking onto Planet-Man; Shrink-Scope's
wooden hand activating size-change energy; Planet-Man shrinking through a
photoreal warped field; he lands human-scale in mission control; she points to
a city-threat display.

## Page 05 Mission Pattern

Story role: teach readers what future stories feel like.
Layout: five panels in a clear reading order.
Caption boxes:
1. "First, Shrink-Scope sees the danger."
2. "Then she sends Planet-Man into the street."
3. "Then his attention wanders."
4. "Then the world pays for every second."
5. "So she keeps him focused."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope at mission control seeing Lord Radius; Planet-Man
walking into damaged city at normal local scale; he pauses fascinated by a
strange tiny weather planet he has made; Radius's pressure rings crack a road;
Shrink-Scope appears on comms or nearby rooftop pointing urgently.

## Page 06 Planet-Fu

Story role: demonstrate Planet-Man's powers in action and Shrink-Scope's role
as focus.
Layout: six action panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "Planet-Fu began as art."
2. "A volcanic planet became a warning shot."
3. "An ice world became a wall."
4. "A ringed planet cut the force field."
5. "A gas giant stole the villain's breath."
6. "Focused, creation became defense."
Visual beats: Planet-Man uses orbiting mini-planets as combat drones against
Lord Radius; realistic fire, ice, ring-blade, gas, and debris effects; civilians
behind an ice shield; Shrink-Scope in one panel guiding him with crystal eyes.

## Page 07 Responsibility

Story role: show the decisive choice and why the mission matters.
Layout: four panels, less spectacle and more consequence.
Caption boxes:
1. "Planet-Man saw the city as more than scenery."
2. "It was a world that kept living after he looked away."
3. "He stopped making something new."
4. "He chose the people already there."
Visual beats: Planet-Man sees civilians, damaged homes, and Shrink-Scope's
exhaustion; mini-planets freeze in their orbits as he concentrates; he forms a
planetary cage construct around Lord Radius; Shrink-Scope prepares her prison.

## Page 08 First Orbit Complete

Story role: resolve the mission and preview future issues.
Layout: five panels, aftermath and HQ closing image.
Caption boxes:
1. "Lord Radius became one more prisoner in Shrink-Scope's tiny cells."
2. "The block survived."
3. "Planet-Man still wanted the next impossible idea."
4. "Now each idea had a mission."
5. "Find the threat. Focus the maker. Save the world."
Visual beats: shrunken Lord Radius inside miniature prison; civilians in the
damaged but standing street; Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope at HQ mission control;
Planet-Man's room with unfinished worlds now organized for missions; final panel
shows both heroes facing a glowing city map, ready for future cases.
