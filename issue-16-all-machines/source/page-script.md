# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, Circuit World's green-white alien light, photoreal lightning, charred leather, dust, wind-blast shockwaves, exposed wires, cybernetic laser VFX, white lily pad glyph light, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Leapona is a thin adult humanoid frog woman with realistic amphibian skin, long limbs, thick yellow webbing between fingers and toes, narrow yellow eyes, a severe frog-like face, and an afro of thick feathery orange hair; she wears a futuristic white leather hop suit with black accents, no readable labels, no logos, now charred, torn, dirty, and scorched after being electrocuted in Issue 15. Bruiser is a massive muscular adult humanoid frog man with green mottled amphibian skin, thick neck, powerful frog legs, large frog hands and feet, a stern frog-like face, a black eye patch, and a futuristic white leather armored hop suit with black accents, slashes, scorch marks, no readable labels, and no logos; beneath his raised eye patch is a new glowing green cybernetic eye with silver metal trim and mechanical rings, replacing the broken red eye. Machine Monarch is a one-inch-tall humanoid black robot villain with glossy black and matte gunmetal armor panels, embedded glowing neon green circuit patterns across his whole body, round glowing blue LED eyes with no pupils, no human face, a jagged crown made from green circuit boards and tiny components, and a royal purple cape made from bundled wires and braided cables; he must remain tiny throughout. Circuit World is a vast living electronic landscape made from oversized circuit boards, black glass plates, copper traces, glowing green pathways, solder-like hills, upright components, processor monoliths, and exposed wire bundles rising from broken terrain like nerves.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo except intentional cover title text, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting. Do not use speaker-name prefixes.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: announce Machine Monarch's takeover of the fallen Hoppers on Circuit World.
Layout: cover image with one strong realistic-photo composition and modest poster clarity, still grounded in candid photo-comic style.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: ALL MACHINES". Spell the brand name exactly as "PLANET-MAN" with one E in "PLANET"; never spell it "PLANEET-MAN". Small caption box: "The god of machines found new servants."
Visual beats: Machine Monarch, still only one inch tall, stands regally on Bruiser's broad shoulder as his purple wire cape whips in storm wind. Bruiser towers below with robotic posture, his black eye patch raised enough to reveal the new glowing green cybernetic eye with silver metal trim. Bruiser must have exactly two arms and exactly two hands total, with no extra hand, stray hand, or fingers near his face or eyepatch. Leapona lies below on the circuit-board ground in a charred white-and-black hop suit, yellow eyes wide with terror, wrists and ankles pinned by living wires. Lightning splits the green-black sky of Circuit World.
