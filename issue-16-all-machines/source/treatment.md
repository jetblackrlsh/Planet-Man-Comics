# Treatment

## Logline

On Circuit World after her defeat, Leapona wakes beneath a changed Bruiser and discovers that Machine Monarch has turned every machine around her, including Bruiser's new cybernetic eye and the living circuitry beneath her, into an extension of his will.

## Central Conflict

Leapona wants to understand how she lost, reclaim control of the fight, and escape before Machine Monarch can finish whatever he began in Issue 15. Machine Monarch wants to demonstrate that his authority now extends beyond ordinary robots into every machine, every wire, and every partly mechanical body. Bruiser becomes the proof: the strongest Hopper warrior is now a remote weapon in Machine Monarch's court.

## Theme

Power worships the system that controls it. Leapona believes strength makes Bruiser nearly divine, but Machine Monarch proves that even strength can become machinery when the right part of it is replaced.

## Emotional Arc

Leapona begins in wounded disbelief, unable to accept that a one-inch robot defeated her. Her anger returns when she recognizes Machine Monarch, but every attempt to reassert dominance is cut off: Bruiser rejects her hand, crushes her breath, exposes his upgraded eye, and blocks her escape. By the end, Leapona is not defeated by pain alone, but by the terror that the entire world has become Machine Monarch's body.

## Ending

Machine Monarch crosses Bruiser's extended tongue like a royal bridge and gives Leapona a direct command. With Bruiser and Leapona both marked for upgrading, Machine Monarch names the final target: Planet-Man. The issue ends on Leapona's widened eyes under a lightning-split sky.
