# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The pages should feel like documentary phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels, even when the action becomes superpowered. Use believable camera perspective, physical textures, natural imperfect framing, Circuit World's green-white alien light, photoreal lightning, wind-blast dust, exposed wires, cybernetic laser VFX, white lily pad glyph light, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays. No speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no speaker-name prefixes, no subtitles, no watermarks, no extra decorative text, and no blank caption boxes.

## Leapona

- Role: wounded divine-race antagonist who wakes into Machine Monarch's control.
- Body: thin adult humanoid frog woman with realistic amphibian skin, long limbs, thick yellow webbing between fingers and toes large enough to catch air like gliding membranes.
- Face and eyes: narrow yellow eyes, severe frog-like face, expressions moving from pain and blurred confusion to disgust, rage, terror, false confidence, and defeat.
- Hair: afro of thick feathery orange hair, physically textured and voluminous, now dusty and windblown.
- Outfit: futuristic white leather hop suit with black accents, fitted for aerial movement, no readable labels, no logos. For this issue the suit is charred, scorched, dirty, and torn from the electrocution in Issue 15.
- Powers: glides and hovers by using her yellow webbing, sweeps her webbed hands to create wind blasts, can trigger a white lily pad glyph escape attempt under herself.
- Issue state: begins on the ground in pain on Circuit World, vision blurred, eyes fluttering open and closed; later restrained by living wires and stunned by Bruiser's green laser.

## Machine Monarch

- Role: one-inch robot tyrant revealing his new command over all machines.
- Scale: only about one inch tall throughout the issue; he must look tiny when standing on Bruiser's shoulder, walking on Bruiser's tongue, or near Leapona's face.
- Body: humanoid black robot with glossy black and matte gunmetal armor panels, strong mechanical build, glowing neon green circuit patterns embedded under transparent black plating across his face, chest, arms, hands, and legs.
- Eyes: round glowing blue LED eyes with no pupils and no human face; eyes glow brighter during the final command.
- Crown: jagged crown made from green circuit boards, exposed microchips, gold contact pins, and tiny solder points.
- Cape: royal purple cape made from bundled wires and braided cables, with copper and green fiber-optic strands visible along the edges; the cape blows in Circuit World's storm wind.
- Expression range: regal calm, villainous grin, primal superiority, menacing smile.
- Issue state: dusty from Issue 15 but triumphant; he has learned to command Circuit World's living wires and Bruiser's repaired cybernetic machinery.

## Bruiser

- Role: the strongest Hopper warrior, now controlled by Machine Monarch through his mechanical eye.
- Body: massive muscular adult humanoid frog man, much broader and heavier than Leapona, with green mottled realistic amphibian skin, thick neck, powerful frog legs, large frog hands and feet, and stern frog-like face.
- Outfit: futuristic white leather bodysuit with black accents, heavier and more armored than Hoppette's, black armored shoulder pads, black utility seams, no readable labels, no logos. The suit is slashed, scorched, and dirty from Issues 14 and 15.
- Eye patch and upgrade: black eye patch over one eye. When raised, it reveals that the broken ruby red cybernetic laser eye has been replaced by a new glowing green cybernetic eye with silver metal trim and mechanical rings.
- Powers and gear: massive strength, long frog tongue, green cybernetic laser blast from the upgraded eye, hop-suit connection to white lily pad glyph travel.
- Issue state: stands upright with robotic obedience and no warmth. Movements should look unnervingly controlled, not emotionally angry.

## Circuit World

A strange living world where the landscape appears made from oversized circuit boards, embedded copper traces, glassy black plates, glowing green pathways, solder-like hills, upright components, processor monoliths, and bundles of exposed wires. It should feel physically present and real, like a photographed macro-electronic terrain scaled into a vast alien landscape. The world is alive or at least responsive to Machine Monarch, so exposed wires should feel like nerves and restraints.

## White Lily Pad Glyph

Photoreal floor magic/technology effect: a glowing white lily pad symbol on the ground, bright enough to cast cold light onto bodies, armor, wires, and circuit rubble. In this issue it appears under Leapona as an attempted escape route, then flickers and vanishes because Machine Monarch's machine control suppresses the hop-suit system.
