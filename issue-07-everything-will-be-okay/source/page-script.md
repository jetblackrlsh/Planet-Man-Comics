# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, believable lighting, real physical materials, photoreal clairvoyant and cosmic-memory VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is a divine humanoid at normal adult human scale with green vine dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin with subtle molten fissures, molten lava eyes, and a fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct miniature planet. Shrink-Scope is an athletic heroic woman with warm medium-brown hair in a practical tied-back bun, glowing white crystal-ball eyes that can shift into prismatic colors, a polished wooden body with visible grain and black ruler-like hash marks, a black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, a black miniskirt covered in silver stars, and glittery black heels. Madame Multiverse appears only as memory/vision: a very frail skinny ethereal alien woman with long silver comet-tail hair, colorful nebula-dust skin, radiant blue starburst eyes, and a patchwork dress stitched from literal miniature universes.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: introduce the quiet emotional issue and the final lie.
Layout: cover image, dramatic candid-photo composition with modest poster clarity. In Shrink-Scope's dim HQ, Planet-Man hugs Shrink-Scope with relieved joy while she looks past his shoulder with hidden tears. Behind them, a crystal lens reflection shows countless tiny planets disappearing into white light.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: EVERYTHING WILL BE OKAY". Small caption box: "One truth was too heavy to share."

## Page 02 Are You Happy?

Story role: open with quiet concern and Shrink-Scope's fear that Planet-Man resents being kept in her world.
Layout: six documentary panels in the HQ common area and mission control doorway.
Caption boxes:
1. "The HQ was quiet after the missions."
2. Shrink-Scope short speech: "Are you happy?"
3. Planet-Man short speech: "Of course. Why?"
4. "She had heard the change in him."
5. Shrink-Scope short speech: "I kept you here."
6. Shrink-Scope short speech: "Are you angry with me?"
Visual beats: Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope seated near the couch and central table; soft night lighting; miniature planet beads on Planet-Man's suit dimly glowing; Shrink-Scope's wooden hands folded tight; his concern sharpening; hallway doors behind them.

Layout: six panels, goodnight sequence into split-bedroom emotional ending.
Caption boxes:

2. Planet-Man short speech: "Good night, Shrink-Scope."
3. Shrink-Scope short speech: "Good night, Planet-Man."
4. "For the first time in a long time, he slept."
5. "In her room, the truth stayed awake."
6. Shrink-Scope short speech: "Everything will be okay."
Visual beats: Planet-Man noticing the time on an abstract unreadable clock display; both walking to separate corridor doors; Planet-Man asleep peacefully in his room among quiet miniature planets; Shrink-Scope curled awake in bed crying silently; prismatic reflections of erased worlds in her tears; final small panel of her rocking under black star-patterned blankets, repeating the line to herself.
