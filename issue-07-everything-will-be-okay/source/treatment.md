# Treatment

## Title

Planet-Man: Everything Will Be Okay

## Logline

In the quiet of Shrink-Scope's HQ, Planet-Man finally confesses that Madame Multiverse's destroyed worlds have been haunting him, and Shrink-Scope makes a merciful lie that saves his peace at the cost of her own.

## Central Conflict

Planet-Man believes he may have committed unknowable mass death when he destroyed Madame Multiverse's multiverse dress. Shrink-Scope wants to help him heal, but her clairvoyance gives her the terrible truth: he is right.

## Theme

Love can comfort honestly, but it can also become a burden when protection turns into deception.

## Emotional Arc

Planet-Man begins guarded and restless, afraid that his home might be built over guilt. Shrink-Scope begins worried that she trapped him in a life he resents. Their conversation reassures him that his stay is now a choice, then reveals the deeper wound. Shrink-Scope's lie gives him visible relief and his first good rest in a long time. The ending transfers the unresolved pain to Shrink-Scope, who repeats that everything will be okay because she needs to believe it.

## Ending

Planet-Man sleeps peacefully in his room, surrounded by quiet miniature worlds. In her own room, Shrink-Scope curls awake in bed, crying over the dead worlds she actually saw and over the lie she chose to tell.

## Cast

- Planet-Man: creator god, normal human scale in Shrink-Scope's world, haunted by the cost of destroying Madame Multiverse's dress.
- Shrink-Scope: heroic clairvoyant and size-changing protector, morally serious, now willing to carry a private truth to preserve Planet-Man's fragile peace.
- Madame Multiverse: absent but central; remembered through cosmic fragments and the destroyed multiverse dress.

## Page Count

8 pages total: cover plus 7 story pages.
