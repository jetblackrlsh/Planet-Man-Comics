# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The pages should feel like documentary phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels, even when the action becomes cosmic memory or clairvoyant vision. Use believable camera perspective, physical textures, natural imperfect framing, photoreal VFX, realistic interior lighting, and clean rectangular caption boxes. No anime, no painted illustration, no cartoon rendering, no comic-book linework, no studio-poster polish except on the cover.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays.

## Planet-Man

- Role: protagonist, emotionally haunted by the battle against Madame Multiverse.
- Body: divine humanoid at normal adult human scale in Shrink-Scope's world, black rocky volcanic skin with rough stone texture and subtle molten fissures.
- Face and eyes: warm but troubled expressions, glowing molten lava eyes, sometimes wet with grief; visible tension in jaw and brow.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, organic and root-like, hanging around his face and shoulders.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct tiny miniature planet with oceans, clouds, rock, ice, fire, rings, forests, metal, or alien terrain.
- Signature power motif: miniature planets hovering near him, dimming or settling when he is emotionally vulnerable.
- Emotional states this issue: performative reassurance, tenderness, dread, trembling confession, sudden joyful relief, peaceful sleep.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: co-lead, emotional anchor, clairvoyant witness, and keeper of the final lie.
- Body: athletic heroic woman with polished wooden body, visible grain, and black ruler-like hash marks across face, arms, torso, and legs.
- Face and eyes: warm medium-brown hair in a practical tied-back bun; glowing white crystal-ball eyes that shift into many prismatic colors during clairvoyant time-sight.
- Outfit: black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, glittery black heels.
- Motifs: crystal optics, microscope shapes, ruler marks, silver stars, black practical superhero gear, polished bark texture, calm heroic posture.
- Emotional states this issue: concern, guilt about having shrunk Planet-Man into this world, careful reassurance, protective resolve, compassionate lying smile, private collapse.

## Madame Multiverse Memory

- Role: remembered source of the trauma, not physically present in current time.
- Visual identifiers: very frail skinny ethereal alien woman with long weightless silver comet-tail hair, colorful nebula-dust skin, radiant blue starburst eyes, and a patchwork dress stitched from literal miniature universes and spacetime fabric.
- Use only in memory/vision panels: cosmic fragments, dying miniature worlds, stitched universe cloth tearing into light.
