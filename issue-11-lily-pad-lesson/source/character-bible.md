# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The pages should feel like documentary phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels, even when the action becomes superpowered. Use believable camera perspective, physical textures, natural imperfect framing, realistic training-room lighting, photoreal action VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes. No anime, no painted illustration, no cartoon rendering, no comic-book linework, no studio-poster polish except modest cover clarity.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays.

## Hoppette

- Role: protagonist-antagonist of the sparring match, a messenger from Planet-Man's other miniature worlds.
- Body: adult humanoid curvy frog woman, athletic and agile, with smooth realistic amphibian skin details and frog-like hands and feet.
- Face and eyes: expressive rounded frog-like face, blue eyes, confident smile that can soften into sincere concern.
- Hair: long curly brown hair that flies outward during rapid hops.
- Outfit: futuristic flexible leather white bodysuit with black accents, fitted practical panels, subtle travel-device seams, no readable labels, no logos.
- Powers: massive frog tongue, adhesive mucus, superhuman leaping, wall-and-ceiling contact, speed-blitz movement, and travel between miniature planets through glowing white lily pad glyphs produced by her suit.
- Mucus continuity: clear glossy adhesive mucus appears first over Shrink-Scope's eyes, then in small splashes after each kick, then as thick strands pinning Shrink-Scope in place. It should look wet and physically sticky, not gore.
- Expression range: friendly thanks, teasing confidence, tactical focus, frustration with unfairness, compassion after winning.
- Silhouette: spring-loaded crouch, long curls, white-and-black bodysuit, frog-like hands and feet, luminous lily pad glyph motif.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: tactical hero, clairvoyant, and the character forced to hear Hoppette's criticism.
- Body: athletic heroic adult woman with polished wood skin, visible grain, and black ruler-like hash marks across face, arms, torso, and legs.
- Hair: warm medium-brown hair pulled back in a practical tied-back bun; never bald.
- Eyes: normally glowing white crystal-ball eyes; during clairvoyance they flare with many colors like refracted crystal, but in this issue they are physically blocked by mucus.
- Outfit: black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, glittery black heels.
- Powers: clairvoyance, near-future combat timing, wooden body, controlled tactical movement, and one size-change per day that is not used here.
- Mucus continuity: starts clean and cocky, then has mucus across her eyes, then across outfit and wooden limbs, then is fully glued in place before Hoppette removes it.
- Expression range: amused confidence, annoyed surprise, pain from a body kick, overwhelmed disorientation, exposed silence, reluctant reflection.
- Silhouette: compact alert stance, crystal eyes, ruler-marked wood body, starry black outfit.

## Miniature-Planet Combat Training Room

- Location: a training room on a different miniature planet, not Shrink-Scope's HQ.
- Visual language: practical padded combat chamber with dark mats, modular barriers, reinforced wall and ceiling strike pads, microscope-like sensor posts with no readable labels, impact scuffs, and a few small observation windows showing a surreal tiny-planet horizon outside.
- Continuity details: clear wall panels and ceiling mats are used for Hoppette's rapid rebound attacks; adhesive mucus accumulates on the room surfaces; a glowing white lily pad glyph appears under both characters on the final page.

## Golem Guardian Program Visual Reference

- The Golem Guardians are humanoid helpers made from fixed collections of miniature planets and planetoids connected by controlled orbital gravity, with tiny clouds, oceans, lava, forests, cities, and rocky pieces forming body parts.
- They should look like Blizzard's construction style, but not ice-specific: clear bulky humanoid silhouettes with head, torso, shoulders, arms, hands, legs, and feet, with many distinct mini-worlds embedded throughout the body and pale root-like gravity filaments binding the planets into muscle and nerve systems.
- Avoid abstract clusters, loose mobile-like orbits, random floating spheres, or shapeless piles. The planets must form readable humanoid anatomy.
- In this issue they appear only as small caption-adjacent visual inserts, not as major characters.
