# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, believable training-room lighting, real physical materials, photoreal action VFX, realistic motion blur, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Hoppette is an adult humanoid curvy frog woman with smooth realistic amphibian skin, blue eyes, expressive rounded frog-like face, long curly brown hair, frog-like hands and feet, athletic spring-loaded posture, and a futuristic flexible leather white bodysuit with black accents, fitted practical panels, subtle travel-device seams, no readable labels, no logos. Shrink-Scope is an athletic heroic adult woman with polished wooden body, visible wood grain, black ruler-like hash marks across face, arms, torso, and legs, warm medium-brown hair in a practical tied-back bun, glowing white crystal-ball eyes that flare with prismatic colors during clairvoyance, a black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, and glittery black heels; Shrink-Scope is never bald.

Setting continuity: a combat training room on a different miniature planet, with dark padded mats, reinforced wall and ceiling strike pads, modular barriers, microscope-like sensor posts with no readable labels, impact scuffs, and small observation windows showing a surreal tiny-planet horizon outside. Hoppette's adhesive mucus is clear, glossy, wet, and sticky-looking; it accumulates over Shrink-Scope's eyes, body, and nearby surfaces without looking bloody or disgusting.

Golem Guardian continuity: when Golem Guardians appear in inserts or as examples, make them clear humanoid guardians made from miniature planets, similar to Blizzard's body construction but not ice-specific. They have head, torso, shoulders, arms, hands, legs, and feet formed from many realistic tiny worlds, with root-like gravity filaments acting as muscles and nerves. Avoid abstract clusters, loose mobile-like orbits, random floating spheres, or shapeless piles.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: announce a contained sparring issue where a friendly training match becomes a hard lesson.
Layout: cover image with one strong realistic-photo composition and modest poster clarity, still grounded in the candid photo-comic style.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: LILY PAD LESSON". Small caption box: "A sparring match becomes a warning."
Visual beats: Hoppette rebounds sideways from a padded training-room wall, hair flying, one frog hand braced against the surface, white-and-black bodysuit catching bright training lights. Shrink-Scope stands below with one arm raised defensively, prismatic crystal eyes partially obscured by glistening mucus. Clear adhesive strands arc through the air. A faint glowing white lily pad glyph is visible on the floor behind them.
