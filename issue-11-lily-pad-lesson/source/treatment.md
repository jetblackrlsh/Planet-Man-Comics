# Treatment

## Logline

On a miniature-world training room, Hoppette blinds and outmaneuvers Shrink-Scope in a sparring match designed less to win a fight than to force an uncomfortable conversation about Planet-Man's divided responsibility.

## Central Conflict

Shrink-Scope believes she has earned confidence because she defeated Hoppette once and helped turn Planet-Man toward care. Hoppette believes that same confidence has hardened into possession: Shrink-Scope's world still receives Planet-Man's attention first, while his other creations receive proxies and promises.

## Theme

Care cannot become ownership. Helping someone become responsible does not give one world a permanent claim over every future act of care.

## Ending

Hoppette frees Shrink-Scope from the mucus, refuses to treat the victory as humiliation, and takes her home through a glowing white lily pad glyph. Shrink-Scope leaves with no easy rebuttal, forced to feel the limits of clairvoyance, strategy, and personal claim.

## Emotional Arc

- Shrink-Scope begins amused and cocky, treating the sparring match as friendly repetition of a fight she already solved.
- Hoppette turns the match with prepared tactics: mucus over the crystal eyes, rapid wall-and-ceiling attacks, and adhesive buildup.
- Once Shrink-Scope is immobilized, the physical defeat becomes a moral argument about Planet-Man's attention and the limits of the Golem Guardian program.
- Shrink-Scope moves from annoyance to reluctant agreement when Hoppette proves, physically and strategically, how easy a fixed toolset can be exploited.
- Hoppette ends with compassion instead of triumph, bringing Shrink-Scope home while making the unresolved truth impossible to ignore.

## Cast

- **Hoppette:** A messenger from another neglected miniature world; agile, sincere, and willing to use a sparring match to make Shrink-Scope listen.
- **Shrink-Scope:** Tactical clairvoyant hero of Planet-Man's chosen mission world; capable and proud, but exposed when her future sight and assumptions are blocked.

## Visual Strategy

The issue is contained almost entirely inside a practical combat training room on another miniature planet. The room should feel physically real: padded walls, ceiling grips, impact mats, modular barriers, and window slits showing the impossible curved horizon of a tiny world outside. The action reads through candid multi-panel page layouts, motion blur, repeated Hoppette silhouettes, colored crystal-eye refractions, and glistening adhesive mucus as a recurring continuity mark.
