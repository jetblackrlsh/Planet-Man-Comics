# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a
complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted
illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism
panels with natural imperfect framing, believable lighting, real physical
materials, photoreal action VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is a divine humanoid with green vine
dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten lava eyes, and a fitted beaded
bodysuit where every bead is a distinct miniature planet. Shrink-Scope is a
heroic woman with warm medium-brown tied-back hair, glowing white crystal-ball
eyes, a polished wooden body with black ruler-like hash marks, a black crop top
with a crystal microscope insignia, a black miniskirt with silver stars, and
glittery black heels. Madame Multiverse is a very frail skinny ethereal alien
woman with long weightless silver comet-tail hair, colorful nebula-dust skin,
radiant blue starburst eyes, and a patchwork dress stitched from literal
miniature universes and spacetime fabric.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no
floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable
decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must
be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or
art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: introduce Madame Multiverse as the cosmic threat and jealous divine
tempter.
Layout: cover image, dramatic candid-photo composition with slight poster
clarity. Madame Multiverse floats above a real damaged city street, silver
comet hair drifting upward, blue starburst eyes glowing, patchwork spacetime
dress alive with tiny stitched universes. Planet-Man flies upward from below
with mini-planets orbiting. Shrink-Scope appears as a small crystal-vision
reflection in the corner.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: MADAME
MULTIVERSE". Small caption box: "A god who wanted him to look away."

## Page 02 Vision Of The Attack

Story role: establish Shrink-Scope's clairvoyant vision and the civilian
stakes.
Layout: five documentary panels in HQ and the city.
Caption boxes:
1. "Shrink-Scope saw the sky fold."
2. "Madame Multiverse had come for attention."
3. "Civilians rose inside tiny ring-shaped universes."
4. Shrink-Scope short speech: "Planet-Man, now."
5. "He launched before the vision ended."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope at crystal lens controls; her glowing eyes filled
with cosmic rings; Madame Multiverse floating over a chaotic street; civilians
trapped inside toroidal miniature universes and lifted upward; Planet-Man
blasting out of HQ toward the city.

## Page 03 Rescue Above The City

Story role: show Planet-Man saving civilians and Madame revealing the bait.
Layout: six fast action panels over city streets and rooftops.
Caption boxes:
1. "Planet-Man tore through the rising rings."
2. "One by one, he pulled people free."
3. "Every rescued life dragged him higher."
4. "The trap had already worked."
5. Madame short speech: "I only needed you to notice me."
6. Planet-Man short speech: "What do you want?"
Visual beats: Planet-Man flying through traffic-level chaos; him breaking open
a toroidal universe and carrying a civilian downward; more civilians rescued on
rooftops; Madame Multiverse watching with serene jealousy; close-up of her
patchwork universe dress; Planet-Man hovering opposite her over the city.

## Page 04 The Lonely Gods

Story role: connect Madame's loneliness to Planet-Man's old emptiness.
Layout: five quieter floating conversation panels above the city.
Caption boxes:
1. "She had lived as a god among mortals."
2. Madame short speech: "Loneliness is all they ever gave me."
3. "Planet-Man knew the shape of that void."
4. Planet-Man short speech: "I made planets to fill it."
5. Madame short speech: "Then create with me forever."
Visual beats: Madame surrounded by tiny universes like luminous jewelry; memory
image of Planet-Man's old shelves of miniature planets; both gods suspended
against clouds and city lights; her hand offering a spinning baby universe; his
expression softened but cautious.

## Page 05 Refusal And Threat

Story role: turn the temptation into a threat against Shrink-Scope.
Layout: six panels, argument sharpening into danger.
Caption boxes:
1. "But Planet-Man was not empty anymore."
2. Planet-Man short speech: "Shrink-Scope gave me purpose."
3. "He offered Madame a better path."
4. Madame short speech: "Mortals waste a god's attention."
5. Planet-Man short speech: "I will never betray her."
6. Madame short speech: "Then I will kill her."
Visual beats: Planet-Man speaking with controlled compassion; brief crystal
image of Shrink-Scope in HQ; Madame's face turning disgusted; her dress
universes brightening dangerously; Planet-Man's molten eyes flaring; Madame's
devilish grin.

## Page 06 Flat Universe Blade

Story role: begin the fight and wound Planet-Man.
Layout: six kinetic combat panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "Rage made him fast."
2. "A flat universe stopped his fist."
3. "It spun into a blade."
4. "He answered with a ringed world."
5. "Universe cut through planet."
6. "The edge found his cheek."
Visual beats: Planet-Man charging with flying punch; Madame blocking with a
floating miniature flat universe shield; flat universe spinning like a saw
disc; Planet-Man launching a spinning ringed miniature planet; the flat
universe cleaving through the ringed planet's sharp ring; Planet-Man barely
dodging, left cheek cut, hand going to the wound.

## Page 07 God Of Universes

Story role: show Madame overpowering Planet-Man and taunting him.
Layout: six bruising action panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "She crossed the distance in a blink."
2. "Her fists struck like collapsing stars."
3. "Ice worlds rose around him."
4. "She shattered every orbit."
5. Madame short speech: "A god of planets cannot beat universes."
6. "Planet-Man smiled anyway."
Visual beats: Madame rapidly flying into close combat; barrage of punches
hitting Planet-Man's rocky body; Planet-Man battered and bruised with cut
cheek; orbiting miniature ice planets forming a defensive shell; her fists
shattering the ice planets; weakened Planet-Man breathing hard with a confident
smirk as Madame narrows her eyes.

## Page 08 Biggest Bang Bomb

Story role: resolve the fight and return emotionally to Shrink-Scope.
Layout: six panels, clear cause and effect from realization to aftermath.
Caption boxes:
1. Planet-Man short speech: "Every universe holds planets."
2. "Her dress was a multiverse."
3. Planet-Man short speech: "Biggest Bang Bomb!"
4. "Every tiny planet answered at once."
5. "Madame Multiverse became light."
6. Planet-Man short speech: "I will always keep you safe."
Visual beats: Planet-Man glaring, battered but focused; Madame's eyes widening
as she understands; the miniature planets inside every stitched universe of her
dress igniting; massive cosmic explosion launching Planet-Man backward and
seemingly atomizing Madame into star-dust; final HQ panel of exhausted
Planet-Man hugging Shrink-Scope tightly; Shrink-Scope supporting him toward his
bedroom with recovery supplies ready.
