# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The images should feel like documentary
phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels. Use
natural lighting where possible, believable physical textures, imperfect
framing, realistic locations, photoreal power effects, and clean rectangular
caption boxes. No anime, no painted illustration, no cartoon rendering, no
comic-book linework, no studio-poster polish except modestly on the cover.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or
cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays.

## Planet-Man

- Role: protagonist, creator god, protector of Shrink-Scope's world.
- Body: divine humanoid with black rocky volcanic skin, rough stone texture,
  subtle molten fissures, and glowing molten lava eyes.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, organic and root-like, hanging around his face
  and shoulders.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit; every bead is a distinct tiny miniature
  planet with oceans, clouds, rock, ice, fire, rings, or forests.
- Powers: Mini-Planetkinesis. He summons and telekinetically controls
  miniature planets as shields, drones, rescue tools, and weapons.
- Injury continuity: after page 5, his left cheek is cut. On pages 6-8, his
  rocky skin and planet-bead suit show bruised stone, dusty cracks, scuffed
  beads, and combat exhaustion.
- Personality: imaginative, lonely but healing, protective, increasingly
  disciplined, capable of terrible force when Shrink-Scope is threatened.
- Motif: orbiting marble-to-boulder-size mini-planets, molten orange glow,
  green vine shapes, dust and debris reacting to gravity-like force.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: co-lead, mission controller, emotional anchor.
- Body: heroic woman with polished wooden body and black ruler-like hash marks
  across skin and limbs.
- Face: focused face with glowing white crystal-ball eyes.
- Hair: warm medium-brown hair pulled back in a practical tied-back style.
- Outfit: black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt
  covered in silver stars, glittery black heels.
- Powers: clairvoyance and the ability to change the size of one thing per
  day. In this issue her clairvoyance reveals the attack at the start.
- Personality: disciplined, direct, brave, morally serious, protective of her
  world, quietly prepared for Planet-Man's recovery.
- Motif: crystal optics, microscope shapes, ruler marks, silver stars, black
  practical superhero gear, mission-control displays.

## Madame Multiverse

- Role: antagonist, lonely cosmic god and jealous suitor.
- Body: very frail, skinny, ethereal alien humanoid frame.
- Hair: long weightless hair resembling the silver tail of a comet, drifting
  upward and outward as if gravity barely affects it.
- Skin: colorful nebula-dust texture with luminous violet, magenta, teal,
  indigo, and gold particulate gradients, physically real rather than painted.
- Eyes: radiant blue starbursts, bright and unsettling.
- Outfit: patchwork dress made of stitched-together miniature universes. The
  dress is literal spacetime fabric, with tiny galaxies, toroidal universes,
  flat universes, starfields, cosmic seams, and visible stitched borders.
- Powers: creates and telekinetically manipulates miniature universes, including
  toroidal universes used as prisons and a spinning flat universe used as a
  blade-like shield and projectile.
- Personality: lonely, possessive, grand, jealous, contemptuous of mortals,
  willing to kill Shrink-Scope to claim Planet-Man's attention.
- Motif: floating donut-shaped universes, blue starburst eyes, silver comet
  hair, stitched multiverse fabric, godlike elegance over real urban disaster.

## Key Locations

- Threatened City Streets: dense urban streets in Shrink-Scope's world,
  emergency vehicles, damaged pavement, frightened civilians, strange cosmic
  VFX composited into real street photography, no readable signs.
- Shrink-Scope HQ Mission Control: functional superhero operations room with
  crystal lens viewing apparatus, microscope-inspired consoles, abstract city
  surveillance displays with no readable text, black surfaces, silver star
  accents, polished wood trim with ruler marks, and serious low lighting.
- Planet-Man HQ Bedroom: human-scale bedroom/workshop with bed, storage,
  shelves of unfinished miniature worlds, black volcanic stone textures, green
  vine-like cables, molten orange accent lights, and recovery supplies.
