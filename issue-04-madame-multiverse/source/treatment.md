# Treatment

## Logline

When Madame Multiverse attacks civilians to draw out Planet-Man, she tempts him
with divine companionship and forces him to prove that responsibility matters
more than loneliness.

## Central Conflict

Madame Multiverse believes gods should reserve their care for other gods.
Planet-Man has begun to believe the opposite: creations do not owe their maker
worship, but the maker still owes them care. Her threat against Shrink-Scope
turns a philosophical temptation into a personal battle.

## Theme

Creation is not ownership. Companionship without conscience is another form of
abandonment.

## Emotional Arc

Planet-Man recognizes a familiar loneliness in Madame Multiverse, but refuses
to let that recognition excuse cruelty. The fight pushes him into a frightening
choice: using the planetary life inside her multiverse dress as a weapon. He
returns shaken, protective, and more aware of what his bond with Shrink-Scope
means.

## Ending

Planet-Man survives the Biggest Bang Bomb and comes back to HQ exhausted. His
hug with Shrink-Scope resolves the central threat visually: he chose the mortal
who gave him purpose over the god who offered escape.
