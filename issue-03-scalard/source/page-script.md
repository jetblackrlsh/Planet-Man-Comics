# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a
complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted
illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism
panels with natural imperfect framing, believable lighting, real physical
materials, photoreal action VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is a divine humanoid with green vine
dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten lava eyes, and a fitted beaded
bodysuit where every bead is a distinct miniature planet. Shrink-Scope is a
heroic woman with warm medium-brown hair pulled back into the same practical
tied-back style shown on the cover and page 2, glowing white crystal-ball eyes,
a polished wooden body with black ruler-like hash marks, a black crop top with
a crystal microscope insignia, a black miniskirt with silver stars, and
glittery black heels.
Scalard is a tall split-faced wizard in an asymmetrical black and white robe:
one face half is deep red with one black devil horn, the other face half is
shimmering white with half of a gold halo; he carries a crooked black-bone and
white-crystal wand.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no
floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable
decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must
be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or
art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: signal Scalard's identity, moral imbalance, and Planet-Man's
temptation.
Layout: cover image, one dramatic candid-photo composition with slight poster
clarity. Scalard stands in the Chamber of Uneven Scales with red-black smoke
behind his horned half and white-gold shimmer behind his halo half. Planet-Man
faces him from below with mini-planets orbiting, one icy mini-planet catching
light near the foreground. Shrink-Scope appears as a small crystal-vision inset
or distorted mission-control reflection.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: SCALARD".
Small caption box: "The wizard who weighed the world toward evil."

## Page 02 The Vision Blinds

Story role: establish Shrink-Scope's blocked clairvoyance and the mission.
Layout: five documentary panels inside Shrink-Scope HQ mission control.
Caption boxes:
1. "Shrink-Scope searched for the next villain."
2. "Her crystal sight hit a wall of smoke."
3. "The interference had a source."
4. Shrink-Scope short speech: "Planet-Man, follow the magic."
5. "He went where her vision could not."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope at crystal lens controls; her glowing eyes reflecting
static-like magical smoke; black and white smoke crossing mission displays; her
wooden hand pointing toward an abstract city map; Planet-Man launching from HQ
with miniature planets orbiting.

## Page 03 The Wizard's Argument

Story role: introduce Scalard's worldview and the moral challenge.
Layout: six panels in Scalard's Chamber of Uneven Scales.
Caption boxes:
1. "Scalard waited inside a crooked hall."
2. Scalard short speech: "You serve the wrong side."
3. "He said balance was a bedtime story."
4. "He said evil was the world's true shape."
5. Planet-Man short speech: "How can wrong become right?"
6. Scalard short speech: "In a world of villains, heroism is the crime."
Visual beats: Planet-Man entering through red-black and white-gold smoke;
Scalard revealed with split face and asymmetrical robe; tilted scales hanging
from the ceiling; Planet-Man studying the wizard, disturbed but engaged;
Scalard's wand tracing uneven symbols with no readable text.

## Page 04 The World Unchosen

Story role: let Planet-Man reject Scalard's claim with the series premise.
Layout: five panels, quieter philosophical exchange with cosmic visual echoes.
Caption boxes:
1. "Planet-Man had shaped worlds before."
2. "Some he made gentle. Some he made cruel."
3. "This world had been left unchosen."
4. Planet-Man short speech: "That means it can still change."
5. "Scalard smiled, because he already knew."
Visual beats: Planet-Man surrounded by small ghostly reflections of worlds he
created; Scalard's magic revealing Planet-Man's godly scale as a looming
shadow behind him; close-up of Scalard recognizing the truth; tilted balance
scale refusing to level; Planet-Man tense as the chamber lights split around
him.

## Page 05 The Offer

Story role: reveal Scalard's bait and the genuine temptation.
Layout: six panels, intimate and tense.
Caption boxes:
1. "The smokescreen had been bait."
2. Scalard short speech: "I can restore your size."
3. "The spell needed his consent."
4. Scalard short speech: "Leave her. Leave this world."
5. "Planet-Man knew the offer was real."
6. "Home had never sounded so heavy."
Visual beats: Scalard holding up a split black-white spell circle; Planet-Man
seen as a towering translucent original-size silhouette beyond the chamber;
Shrink-Scope's tiny distorted image flickering in smoke; the wand hovering near
Planet-Man but not touching him; Planet-Man staring at his planet-bead suit,
tempted and unsettled.

## Page 06 Worth Focusing On

Story role: show Planet-Man's decisive emotional choice.
Layout: five panels, memory fragments resolving into commitment.
Caption boxes:
1. "He remembered the shelf."
2. "Worlds gathered dust where wonder used to be."
3. "Running away had not made him free."
4. Planet-Man short speech: "I am done abandoning my creations."
5. "Scalard raised his wand."
Visual beats: dim memory panel of Planet-Man's old room full of miniature
worlds on dusty shelves; lonely close-up of molten eyes; abandoned worlds with
cobwebbed containment rings; Planet-Man back in the chamber, standing taller
and focused; Scalard's split face hardening as his wand ignites.

## Page 07 Polymorph Volley

Story role: stage the magical combat and Planet-Man's struggle to stay focused.
Layout: six fast action panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "Polymorph light tore through the air."
2. "Planet-Man threw worlds into orbit."
3. "One became a frog."
4. "One became a rabbit."
5. "One became a squirrel."
6. "Then the shockwave broke his charge."
Visual beats: Scalard firing multiple beams from his wand; Planet-Man's
mini-planets orbiting as shields; a greenish mini-planet transforming into a
frog midair; an icy mini-planet transforming into a rabbit; a ringed
mini-planet transforming into a squirrel; Planet-Man charging and then being
violently knocked backward by a white-gold and red-black shockwave.

## Page 08 Reflected Order

Story role: resolve the fight and the emotional arc back at HQ.
Layout: six panels, clear cause and effect from defeat to return.
Caption boxes:
1. Scalard short speech: "I preserve the true order."
2. "He charged one final spell."
3. "Planet-Man called up an ice world."
4. "The spell found its reflection."
5. "Scalard became harmless."
6. Planet-Man short speech: "I found something worth focusing on."
Visual beats: battered Planet-Man on the cracked floor as Scalard approaches;
the wand firing a brilliant polymorph bolt; Planet-Man summoning a reflective
mini ice planet between himself and the spell; the beam bouncing back into
Scalard; tiny split-colored mouse with a fallen crooked wand where Scalard
stood; final panel back in HQ, Shrink-Scope's crystal eyes clear again as
Planet-Man smiles gently beside her.
