Issue three villain: Scalard.

Scalard is a wizard who wears an asymmetrical black and white robe. Half of
his face is red with a black devil horn and the other half is shimmering white
with half of a gold halo. He is obsessed with the natural order of things. He
believes that balance is an illusion, and that the true nature of the world is
evil. He uses magic to keep the scales imbalanced in favor of villainy.

Shrink-Scope uses clairvoyance to find the next villain, but magical
interference blocks her vision. She feels the source and dispatches Planet-Man.
Planet-Man finds Scalard, who argues that Planet-Man is on the wrong side and
should join the villains. Planet-Man questions how anyone could justify evil.
Scalard argues that in a world of villains, it is wrong to be a hero.

Planet-Man considers the idea but rejects it. When he creates worlds, he can
choose their alignment. He did not choose one for this world, so it can change
and evolve in either direction. Scalard's magic lets him recognize Planet-Man
as the god he claims to be and also as an intruder who does not belong on their
world. Scalard reveals that his mystical smokescreen was bait.

Scalard offers a genuine spell that can return Planet-Man to his original size
and let him leave the world, but only if Planet-Man consents and stops helping
Shrink-Scope. If Planet-Man refuses, Scalard will try to erase him. Planet-Man
remembers the loneliness of his old life, the aimless creative spirals, and the
worlds he left collecting dust. He refuses to run away from his creations.

Scalard attacks with polymorph spells. Planet-Man blocks with orbiting
mini-planets; several are transformed into a frog, rabbit, and squirrel. When
Planet-Man charges, Scalard knocks him back with a magical shockwave. Scalard
prepares a final polymorph spell, but Planet-Man summons a reflective mini ice
planet. The spell bounces back and transforms Scalard into a harmless little
mouse.

Back at HQ, Shrink-Scope confirms her clairvoyant visions are clear again. She
notices Planet-Man's focus has improved. Planet-Man smiles and says his focus
has improved because he finally found something worth focusing on.
