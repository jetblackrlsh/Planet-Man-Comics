# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The images should feel like documentary
phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels. Use
natural lighting where possible, believable physical textures, imperfect
framing, realistic locations, and photoreal power effects. No anime, no painted
illustration, no cartoon rendering, no comic-book linework, no studio-poster
polish except modestly on the cover.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or
cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays.

## Planet-Man

- Role: protagonist, creator god, reluctant field operative becoming a focused
  protector.
- Body: divine humanoid with black rocky volcanic skin, rough stone texture,
  subtle molten fissures, and glowing molten lava eyes.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, organic and root-like, hanging around his face
  and shoulders.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit; every bead is a distinct tiny miniature
  planet with oceans, clouds, rock, ice, fire, rings, or forests.
- Powers: Mini-Planetkinesis. He summons and telekinetically controls
  miniature planets as shields, drones, and tactical tools. In this issue he
  creates orbiting shields and a reflective mini ice planet.
- Injury continuity: after page 6, his rocky skin and planet-bead suit show
  scuffs, bruised stone, dusty cracks, and several shaken or missing planet
  beads.
- Personality: imaginative, reflective, restless, vulnerable to distraction,
  but increasingly capable of disciplined heroic focus.
- Motif: orbiting marble-to-boulder-size mini-planets, molten orange glow,
  green vine shapes, real dust and debris reacting to gravity-like force.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: co-lead and mission controller.
- Body: heroic woman with polished wooden body and black ruler-like hash marks
  across skin and limbs.
- Face: focused face with glowing white crystal-ball eyes.
- Hair: warm medium-brown hair pulled back into the same practical tied-back
  style shown on the cover and page 2; do not change it to loose black hair or
  a different color.
- Outfit: black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt
  covered in silver stars, glittery black heels.
- Powers: clairvoyance and the ability to change the size of one thing per
  day. In this issue her clairvoyance is blocked by Scalard's smokescreen.
- Personality: disciplined, direct, brave, morally serious, pragmatic,
  protective of her world.
- Motif: crystal optics, microscope shapes, ruler marks, silver stars, black
  practical superhero gear, miniature prison, mission-control displays.

## Scalard

- Role: antagonist, philosophical wizard-villain, manipulator of moral and
  magical imbalance.
- Body: adult humanoid wizard, tall and severe, with unsettling formal posture.
- Face: exactly split down the center. One half is deep red with a single black
  devil horn and a dark predatory eye. The other half is shimmering white with
  a single half gold halo above it and a pale luminous eye.
- Outfit: asymmetrical black and white wizard robe. The black side is heavy,
  matte, angular, and shadowed. The white side is shimmering, pearlescent, and
  ceremonial. The seam between sides is jagged rather than clean.
- Prop: crooked wand made of blackened bone and white crystal fused together,
  with a tiny uneven balance-scale charm near the grip.
- Powers: clairvoyance-blocking smokescreen, true-recognition magic,
  teleport-like light, polymorph beams, magical shockwaves, and a consent-based
  spell that could restore Planet-Man to original size.
- Personality: calm, absolute, articulate, predatory, convinced that evil is
  not a choice but the true structure of the world.
- Motif: tilted scales, split red-black and white-gold light, magical smoke,
  warped symmetry, beams of transformation light, pages of old spellwork with
  no readable writing.

## Transformed Animals

- Frog: a miniature planet transformed by Scalard's spell; it still has faint
  cloud bands and tiny glowing fissures as a visual echo of the planet it was.
- Rabbit: another transformed mini-planet; small and startled, with subtle icy
  sparkles in its fur.
- Squirrel: another transformed mini-planet; quick, nervous, with a ringed
  tail silhouette that recalls the original ringed world.
- Mouse: Scalard after his own reflected spell hits him; harmless tiny mouse
  with one red-black side and one pale-white side in miniature, plus a tiny
  crooked wand fallen beside him.

## Key Locations

- Shrink-Scope HQ Mission Control: functional superhero operations room with
  crystal lens viewing apparatus, microscope-inspired consoles, abstract city
  surveillance displays with no readable text, black surfaces, silver star
  accents, polished wood trim with ruler marks, and serious low lighting.
- Scalard's Chamber of Uneven Scales: abandoned civic hall converted into a
  wizard's ritual chamber. The room has cracked marble floors, tilted balance
  scales, black-and-white drapery, candles, old books with no readable text,
  suspended red-black smoke on one side, white-gold shimmer on the other, and
  a central ritual circle where the floor slopes subtly out of level.
