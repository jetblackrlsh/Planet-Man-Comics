# Treatment

## Title

Planet-Man: Scalard

## Logline

When a split-faced wizard blocks Shrink-Scope's clairvoyance, Planet-Man
confronts a villain who offers him the one thing he has wanted since being
shrunk: a way home, at the cost of abandoning the world he created.

## Central Conflict

Scalard believes evil is the true natural order and wants Planet-Man to stop
disturbing that order. Planet-Man must decide whether responsibility to his
creation matters more than escape from it.

## Theme

A creator cannot hide behind neutrality when the living world he made is being
shaped by deliberate harm.

## Ending

Planet-Man defeats Scalard by reflecting the wizard's own polymorph spell back
at him, turning the imbalance-maker into a harmless mouse. Back at HQ,
Shrink-Scope's visions return, and Planet-Man admits that his focus has
improved because he has found something worth focusing on.

## Emotional Arc

Planet-Man begins the issue tempted by the idea that this world can be left
behind, because Scalard can truly send him back to his former life. He remembers
that old life as lonely and hollow, full of worlds made and abandoned. By the
end, he chooses active responsibility over escape, and his power becomes more
focused because his attention has finally attached to a purpose.

## Cast

- Planet-Man: creator god, normal-size field hero in Shrink-Scope's world,
  still learning responsibility.
- Shrink-Scope: clairvoyant hero and mission controller whose visions are
  blocked by Scalard's magic.
- Scalard: asymmetrical wizard-villain obsessed with preserving evil as the
  world's natural order.

## Visual Spine

The issue moves from Shrink-Scope's black, crystal-lit HQ to Scalard's tilted
occult chamber of uneven scales. White-gold and red-black magic divide the
visual field around Scalard. Planet-Man's mini-planets provide physical,
grounded contrast to Scalard's slippery transformation spells.
