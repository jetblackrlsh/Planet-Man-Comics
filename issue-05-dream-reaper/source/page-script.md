# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a
complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted
illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism
panels with natural imperfect framing, believable lighting, real physical
materials, photoreal action VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is a divine humanoid with green vine
dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten lava eyes, and a fitted beaded
bodysuit where every bead is a distinct miniature planet. Shrink-Scope is a
heroic woman with warm medium-brown tied-back hair, glowing white
crystal-ball eyes, a polished wooden body with black ruler-like hash marks, a
black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, a black miniskirt with
silver stars, and glittery black heels. The Grim Reaper is a tall figure in a
black hooded robe that completely obscures the body: pitch-black darkness
inside the hood, extremely long sleeves, black gloves, a long robe hiding the
feet, and a large silver-bladed scythe.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no
floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable
decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must
be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or
art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: announce the dream-horror conflict and show Planet-Man facing a
deathly version of his own guilt.
Layout: cover image, dramatic candid-photo composition with modest poster
clarity. Planet-Man floats in a glowing dream realm filled with exotic
miniature planets while the faceless Grim Reaper looms behind him with a scythe
cutting through one small planet. At the bottom edge, a faint inset reflection
shows Planet-Man asleep in his HQ bed.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: DREAM
REAPER". Small caption box: "A dream came to collect."

## Page 02 The Dream Opens

Story role: establish that Planet-Man is asleep, then transition into the dream
realm.
Layout: five panels, slow cinematic zoom and pullback.
Caption boxes:
1. "After the multiverse burned, sleep came late."
2. "In his room, unfinished worlds kept orbit."
3. "The dream found his face."
4. "His eyes opened somewhere impossible."
5. "Every planet there felt alive."
Visual beats: Planet-Man asleep in his HQ bedroom, surrounded by shelves and
containment rings of miniature worlds; closer view of his sleeping rocky face;
extreme close-up of molten eyes beneath closed lids; match-cut to those eyes
opening; pullback reveals him floating in a colorful glowing dream realm among
exotic miniature planets.

## Page 03 The Reaper's Accusation

Story role: introduce the Grim Reaper and the accusation that Planet-Man stole
Death's title.
Layout: six panels, wonder turning to threat.
Caption boxes:
1. "One little world called to him."
2. "The scythe reached it first."
3. Reaper short speech: "When did you decide to steal my job?"
4. "There was nothing inside the hood."
5. Planet-Man short speech: "What do you mean?"
6. Reaper short speech: "Don't play dumb with me!"
Visual beats: Planet-Man reaching toward a beautiful miniature planet; the Grim
Reaper's scythe cutting the planet cleanly in half; Reaper looming in faceless
black hood and long sleeves; Planet-Man recoiling in disturbed confusion;
Reaper dropping into battle stance; Reaper charging forward with scythe raised.

## Page 04 Ringed Worlds And Death's Record

Story role: show the first exchange and reveal why the Reaper is hunting him.
Layout: six kinetic combat panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "Ringed planets snapped into orbit."
2. "Their blades caught the scythe."
3. "Dark magic broke the circle."
4. Reaper short speech: "I earned Death by claiming the most lives."
5. Reaper short speech: "You broke my record."
6. Reaper short speech: "So I have to kill you."
Visual beats: Planet-Man summoning ringed miniature planets around his body;
sharp planetary rings parrying scythe slashes; sparks and cosmic debris in
handheld action panels; Reaper releasing a black shockwave; Planet-Man launched
backward through glowing dream mist; close-up of Planet-Man's eyes widening in
horror as the Reaper explains the destroyed miniature multiverse.

## Page 05 Nightmare Horde

Story role: force Planet-Man to defend himself while refusing the title god of
Death.
Layout: six panels, monster charge and planet wall transformation.
Caption boxes:
1. "The Reaper raised one black glove."
2. "Nightmares poured out of the dark."
3. "Planet-Man built a wall of worlds."
4. Planet-Man short speech: "I didn't know they were living worlds!"
5. Planet-Man short speech: "I am a God of Creation!"
6. "The wall became a cage."
Visual beats: Reaper summoning a horde of living shadow monsters; monsters
charging with claws and fangs; Planet-Man forming a wall of boulder-sized
miniature planets between himself and the horde; monsters clawing and biting
at the planet wall; Planet-Man shouting with pride and distress; the
boulder-sized planets reshaping into a spherical cage around the nightmare
army.

## Page 06 Creation Under Judgment

Story role: make Planet-Man's defense morally ambiguous and escalate the Reaper
into a storm giant.
Layout: six panels, explosion to lightning storm.
Caption boxes:
1. "Every trapped core detonated."
2. "The nightmares vanished screaming."
3. Reaper short speech: "Does that look like creation?"
4. "Then Death grew larger than the sky."
5. Reaper short speech: "Nothing will take my title."
6. "Metal planets drank the lightning."
Visual beats: the cores of each boulder planet inside the cage glowing and
detonating; shadow monsters annihilated in a contained blast; Reaper taunting
Planet-Man from behind drifting smoke; Reaper growing into a massive giant with
storm clouds spiraling overhead; black lightning bolts raining around
Planet-Man; Planet-Man creating metal miniature planets away from himself as
lightning rods that draw the black lightning.

## Page 07 Lucid Creation

Story role: Planet-Man realizes he is dreaming, becomes lucid, and turns the
Reaper's power into his own created answer.
Layout: six panels, realization and reversal.
Caption boxes:
1. Planet-Man short speech: "How are you this powerful?"
2. "Then he felt the bed beneath the world."
3. Planet-Man short speech: "I am dreaming."
4. Planet-Man short speech: "Let me show you how a God Of Creation gets things done!"
5. "Black lightning learned new shapes."
6. "The soldiers carried Death away."
Visual beats: Planet-Man staring up through lightning at the enormous Reaper;
subtle overlay of his real sleeping face and bed beneath the dream realm;
Planet-Man's eyes widening with realization; he shimmers with cosmic sparkling
purple light; falling black lightning reshapes into giant flying soldiers made
of black electricity; the soldiers grab the giant Reaper's cloak and lift him
away into the distance.

## Page 08 The Face Under The Hood

Story role: reveal the Reaper as Planet-Man's dark mirror and end with the
real-world wake-up.
Layout: six panels, nightmare reveal into quiet aftermath.
Caption boxes:
1. "The hood fell."
2. "Death wore Planet-Man's face."
3. "He woke screaming."
4. Shrink-Scope short speech: "It was just a dream."
5. "She held him until the room stopped spinning."
6. Planet-Man short speech: "No. It was real."
Visual beats: Reaper being carried away by black lightning soldiers; his hood
falling back to reveal Planet-Man's own rocky volcanic face, green vine
dreadlocks, and molten eyes; hard cut to Planet-Man sitting upright in bed in a
cold sweat, screaming; Shrink-Scope racing into his HQ bedroom with concern;
Shrink-Scope hugging him tightly at bedside; final close-up of Planet-Man
looking terrified and certain.
