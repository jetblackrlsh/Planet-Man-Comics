# Treatment

## Logline

After destroying Madame Multiverse's living dress worlds, Planet-Man dreams of
being hunted by the Grim Reaper, who claims Planet-Man has stolen the title of
god of Death.

## Central Conflict

Planet-Man believes he is a god of creation, but the Grim Reaper forces him to
face the deaths caused by his victory in the previous issue. The battle is both
a supernatural attack and a nightmare trial over what Planet-Man's power really
means.

## Theme

Creation without awareness can still destroy. The difference is not innocence,
but what Planet-Man chooses once he understands the cost.

## Emotional Arc

Planet-Man begins asleep and unaware, awakens inside a beautiful dream, then
becomes horrified as beauty turns into accusation. He moves from confusion to
defensiveness, then to lucid agency: if the dream is made from his fear, he can
create a response that does not simply imitate Death. The final reveal turns
the Reaper into Planet-Man's own face, leaving the threat unresolved when he
wakes.

## Ending

Shrink-Scope comforts Planet-Man in the real HQ bedroom, but his final line
refuses easy relief. The dream has exposed a consequence from Issue 4 that may
be more than guilt: "No. It was real."
