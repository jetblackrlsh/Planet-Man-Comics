Issue 5 takes place inside one of Planet-Man's dreams. The issue opens with an
establishing shot of Planet-Man sleeping in his HQ bedroom, then zooms into his
face. The camera pulls back from that same face as his eyes open in a colorful,
ethereal, glowing dream realm surrounded by exotic miniature planets.

One miniature planet catches Planet-Man's eye. Before he can reach it, the
planet is cut in half by the scythe of the Grim Reaper. The Grim Reaper wears
a black hooded robe that completely obscures him: pitch-black darkness inside
the hood, extremely long sleeves, black gloves, and a long robe that hides his
feet. He aggressively asks when Planet-Man decided he was going to steal his
job. Planet-Man is disturbed and confused, and asks what he means.

The Grim Reaper charges while yelling, "Don't play dumb with me!" Planet-Man
summons ringed planets to orbit around him, using their sharp rings to parry
the Reaper's scythe slashes. The Reaper releases a shockwave of dark magic
that launches Planet-Man backward.

The Grim Reaper explains that he earned his title as the god of Death because
he claimed more lives than anyone else to ever live. By destroying an entire
miniature multiverse in the previous issue, when he destroyed Madame
Multiverse's dress, Planet-Man broke that record. To keep his title, the Grim
Reaper has to kill Planet-Man.

The Reaper summons a horde of nightmare monsters made of living shadows.
Planet-Man summons a wall of boulder-sized planets between himself and the
charging monsters. As the monsters claw and bite the wall, Planet-Man says he
did not know Madame Multiverse's dress was full of living worlds and that he
does not want to be the God of Death. He insists with pride that he has always
been and always will be a god of creation.

Planet-Man reshapes the boulder planet wall into a cage around the nightmare
army, then detonates the core of each planet in the cage to annihilate the
monsters. The Grim Reaper taunts him by asking whether killing those monsters
seems like something a God of Creation would do.

The Grim Reaper grows into a massive giant as menacing storm clouds spiral
above him. Black lightning rains all around Planet-Man while the Reaper
proclaims that nothing will stop him from reclaiming his title. Planet-Man
creates metal miniature planets away from himself as lightning rods. He
questions how the Reaper can be this powerful, then realizes where the battle
is taking place: he is dreaming.

Now lucid, Planet-Man says, "Let me show you how a God Of Creation gets things
done!" He shimmers with cosmic sparkling purple light and reshapes the falling
black lightning into giant flying soldiers made of black lightning. They grab
the Grim Reaper's cloak and carry him away. As the Reaper is lifted away, his
hood falls down, revealing Planet-Man's face underneath.

Planet-Man wakes in a cold sweat, screaming in his bed. Shrink-Scope races in
with concern, hugs him, and tells him it was just a dream. Planet-Man looks at
her and says, "No. It was real."
