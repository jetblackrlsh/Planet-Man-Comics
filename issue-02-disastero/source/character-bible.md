# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The images should feel like documentary phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels. Natural lighting, believable physical textures, imperfect framing, realistic locations, and photoreal power effects. No anime, no painted illustration, no cartoon rendering, no comic-book linework, no studio-poster polish except modestly on the cover.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays.

## Planet-Man

- Role: protagonist, creator god, reluctant field operative.
- Body: divine humanoid with black rocky volcanic skin, rough stone texture, and subtle molten fissures.
- Face: angular stone face, molten lava eyes glowing orange, intense but often curious expression.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, organic and root-like, hanging around his face and shoulders.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit; every bead is a distinct tiny miniature planet with oceans, clouds, rock, ice, fire, rings, or forests.
- Powers: Mini-Planetkinesis. He summons and telekinetically controls miniature planets; in this issue he uses flight, an opposing-spin gas planet, and Terra Trojan Buckshot.
- Injury continuity: after page 6, his rocky skin and planet-bead suit show burns, bruised stone, ash, cracked beads, and torn glowing fissures.
- Personality: imaginative, reflective, restless, sometimes distracted, but capable of focused lethal resolve when the world is at stake.
- Motif: orbiting marble-to-boulder-size mini-planets, molten orange glow, green vine shapes, real-world dust and debris reacting to gravity-like force.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: co-lead and mission controller.
- Body: heroic woman with polished wooden body, black ruler-like hash marks across skin and limbs.
- Face: focused face with glowing white crystal-ball eyes, serious expression.
- Outfit: black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, glittery black heels.
- Powers: clairvoyance and the ability to change the size of one thing per day.
- Personality: disciplined, direct, brave, morally serious, pragmatic, deeply protective of her world.
- Motif: crystal optics, microscope shapes, ruler marks, silver stars, black practical superhero gear, miniature prison.

## Disastero

- Role: antagonist, existential disaster tyrant.
- Body: towering humanoid composed of natural disasters and catastrophic elements.
- Head: a transparent roaring mass of water shaped like a head, with glowing cold eyes visible inside the water.
- Hair: a crest of water rising backward like a frozen-in-motion tidal wave.
- Arms: two spinning tornado columns forming his shoulders, arms, and hands, with debris spiraling inside the wind.
- Torso: cracked gray stone chest and abdomen like a shattered mountain cliff, with deep fissures and dust.
- Legs: flowing lava legs with black crust plates, molten orange cracks, smoking footprints, and heat shimmer.
- Powers: creates and manipulates natural disasters: lava plumes, tornadoes, earthquakes, storm pressure, and rupturing planetary cores.
- Personality: calm, cruel, entitled, theatrical, absolutely convinced that might makes right and worship is proof of godhood.
- Motif: Apocalypse Church throne hall, storm light, offerings, fearful world leaders, cracked floors, lava glow, cyclone motion, floodwater reflections.

## Key Locations

- Apocalypse Church Exterior: a massive castle-cathedral on a blasted volcanic plain under a violent storm sky, with flooded courtyards, cracked stone causeways, lava trenches, cyclone clouds, and processions of world leaders carrying offerings.
- Apocalypse Church Throne Hall: vast cathedral-castle chamber with high black stone arches, a throne like a disaster altar, offerings from nations, stormwater dripping from the ceiling, lava fissures in the floor, and wind whipping banners with no readable text.
- Shrink-Scope HQ Mission Control: functional superhero operations room with city maps or abstract surveillance displays, crystal lens viewing apparatus, microscope-inspired control consoles, black surfaces with silver star accents, polished wood trim with ruler-like hash marks, and serious low lighting.
