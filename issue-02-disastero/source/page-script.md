# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, believable lighting, real physical materials, photoreal action VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is a divine humanoid with green vine dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten lava eyes, and a fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct miniature planet. Shrink-Scope is a heroic woman with glowing white crystal-ball eyes, a polished wooden body with black ruler-like hash marks, a black crop top with a crystal microscope insignia, a black miniskirt with silver stars, and glittery black heels. Disastero is a towering disaster-bodied super-villain: transparent water head, tidal-wave hair, spinning tornado arms, cracked gray stone torso, flowing lava legs, cold glowing eyes, smoking footprints.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: signal the issue identity, Disastero's scale, and Planet-Man's impossible mission.
Layout: cover image, one dramatic candid-photo composition with slight poster clarity. Disastero looms over the Apocalypse Church castle-cathedral while Planet-Man hovers small but defiant before him, mini-planets orbiting from his bodysuit. Shrink-Scope watches from a mission display inset or distant rooftop silhouette.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: DISASTERO". Small caption box: "A walking apocalypse demands worship."

## Page 02 The Untouchable

Story role: establish Disastero's power, social order, and why nobody challenges him.
Layout: five documentary panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "Disastero could end the world."
2. "So the world learned to kneel."
3. "Leaders brought offerings to the Apocalypse Church."
4. "Those who challenged him lost everything."
5. "He called fear worship."
Visual beats: stormy exterior of Apocalypse Church; world leaders with offerings crossing a cracked causeway; Disastero on a disaster altar; ruined homes under flood, fire, and tornado damage; close-up of Disastero's water head and tidal-wave hair.

## Page 03 The Risk

Story role: show Shrink-Scope's fear and Planet-Man's decision to try conversation.
Layout: five panels inside Shrink-Scope HQ mission control, ending at the castle approach.
Caption boxes:
1. "Shrink-Scope had never tried to shrink him."
2. "Even small, Disastero might still end everything."
3. "Planet-Man asked for one conversation."
4. "Godhood had always felt lonely to him."
5. "He thought loneliness might be a door."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope studying disaster projections through crystal optics; a tiny prison box left unused; Planet-Man surrounded by orbiting mini-planets in quiet reflection; Shrink-Scope reluctantly sending him; Planet-Man flying toward the Apocalypse Church storm.

## Page 04 God To God

Story role: stage the philosophical confrontation before combat.
Layout: six panels in the Apocalypse Church throne hall.
Caption boxes:
1. Disastero short speech: "What have you brought me?"
2. Planet-Man short speech: "Only a question."
3. "Planet-Man spoke of godhood without company."
4. "Of creation filling empty space."
5. Planet-Man short speech: "Do you destroy to fill yours?"
6. Disastero short speech: "Do not compare us."
Visual beats: Disastero mistaking Planet-Man for another supplicant; Planet-Man hovering in the vast throne hall; offerings piled near the disaster altar; mini-planets orbiting Planet-Man like emotional space; Disastero's tornado arm scattering offerings in contempt.

## Page 05 No God Without Worship

Story role: make clear Disastero is not misunderstood and only respects domination.
Layout: five panels, tense close-range argument.
Caption boxes:
1. Disastero short speech: "Where is your church?"
2. Disastero short speech: "Where are your offerings?"
3. Disastero short speech: "Where are your followers?"
4. "Planet-Man found no loneliness there."
5. Planet-Man short speech: "Then I have to kill you."
Visual beats: Disastero looming from water, wind, stone, and lava; Planet-Man's expression hardening; the throne hall floor cracking under Disastero; world leaders cowering; final panel of Planet-Man lowering into a fighting stance with mini-planets aligning.

## Page 06 Walking Apocalypse

Story role: show Disastero overpowering Planet-Man and creating the low point.
Layout: six action panels.
Caption boxes:
1. "Lava rose like judgment."
2. "Planet-Man flew between the plumes."
3. "A tornado hammered him downward."
4. "An opposing gas world broke the spin."
5. "Then Disastero ruptured the suit-worlds."
6. Disastero short speech: "I destroy any planet you create."
Visual beats: lava plumes erupting from the cathedral floor; Planet-Man darting through the air; a tornado funnel from above pushing him down; Planet-Man summoning a mini gaseous planet with opposite swirl; Disastero destabilizing cores in the planet beads on Planet-Man's bodysuit; explosion hurling Planet-Man across the throne hall, bruised and burned.

## Page 07 Terra Trojan Buckshot

Story role: reveal Planet-Man's focused tactic and defeat Disastero.
Layout: five panels, clear cause and effect.
Caption boxes:
1. "Weakly, Planet-Man raised one last world."
2. "Disastero ruptured its core before impact."
3. "Planet-Man had built that world to break."
4. "Every fragment was still his."
5. Planet-Man short speech: "Terra Trojan Buckshot."
Visual beats: injured Planet-Man telekinetically hurling a boulder-sized mini-planet; Disastero casually rupturing it mid-flight; the mini-planet shattering into many smaller mini-planetoids; Planet-Man's glowing eyes focusing; a barrage of mini-planetoid bullets peppering Disastero's water head, tornado arms, cracked stone torso, and lava legs until he collapses.

## Page 08 Removed From The Board

Story role: resolve the conflict and land the emotional consequence.
Layout: five panels, aftermath inside Apocalypse Church and mission control.
Caption boxes:
1. "The walking apocalypse fell silent."
2. "One terror was gone."
3. Shrink-Scope short speech: "You stayed focused."
4. "Planet-Man wished that felt like victory."
5. "Some gods leave only absence behind."
Visual beats: Disastero's elemental body extinguished and broken across the cracked throne hall; world leaders staring in stunned relief; Shrink-Scope arriving or appearing on a crystal comms image impressed; Planet-Man standing bruised among still-orbiting mini-planets; final quiet panel of Planet-Man leaving the Apocalypse Church under clearing storm light, disappointed rather than triumphant.
