# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The pages should feel like documentary phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels. Use believable camera perspective, physical textures, natural imperfect framing, late-night practical HQ lighting, dust, impact rubble, laser scorch damage, cracked floor, cracked wall, bruises, pain, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays. No speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no speaker-name prefixes, no subtitles, no watermarks, no extra decorative text, and no blank caption boxes. This issue contains unusually important dialogue, so the exact quoted dialogue in the page script must appear verbatim inside caption boxes generated directly in the art.

## Planet-Man

- Role: battered hero who wakes near the end and steadies the team.
- Body: adult divine humanoid at normal human scale inside Shrink-Scope's world, black rocky volcanic skin with grounded stone texture.
- Face and eyes: molten lava eyes that glow orange when awake; fully closed while unconscious; bruised and battered throughout.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, dusty and loose from the fight.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct tiny miniature planet; no readable labels or logos.
- Issue state: unconscious on the damaged HQ floor for most of the issue, battered and bruised with eyes closed; wakes on the final page still injured but standing with quiet confidence.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: first to recover, protective of Planet-Man, furious at Hoppette.
- Body: heroic adult woman with polished wooden body covered in black ruler-like hash marks.
- Hair: long brown hair pulled back behind her head in a practical tied-back style; never bald.
- Face and eyes: glowing white crystal-ball eyes when awake; eyes blurred and unfocused when she first wakes; visible bruise on her forehead.
- Outfit: black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, glittery black heels; no readable labels or logos.
- Issue state: starts unconscious beside the cracked floor where her head slammed down; wakes groggy, then becomes sharp and accusatory.

## Hoppette

- Role: injured messenger whose backstory reframes the Hoppers' attack.
- Body: adult humanoid curvy frog woman with smooth realistic amphibian skin, blue eyes, expressive rounded frog-like face, long curly brown hair, frog-like hands and feet.
- Outfit: futuristic flexible white leather hop suit with black accents, fitted practical panels, subtle travel-device seams, no readable labels, and no logos.
- Issue state: starts unconscious with her back against the cracked wall; a dark scorch mark burns across the suit where Bruiser's cybernetic laser shot her; wakes dizzy, coughing, stumbling, and clutching the injury.
- Expression range: pain, panic, defensiveness, regret, annoyance, solemn confession, defeat.

## Bruiser

- Role: absent threat and subject of the conversation.
- Body: massive muscular adult humanoid frog man, much broader and heavier than Hoppette, with green mottled amphibian skin, black eye patch, armored white-and-black hop suit, and cybernetic laser eye.
- Issue state: not physically present in this issue after the cover reference; he has departed for Circuit World with Machine Monarch.

## Machine Monarch

- Role: absent threat whose departure leaves the room broken.
- Body: one-inch humanoid black robot villain with neon green circuit patterns, blue LED eyes, circuit-board crown, and purple wire cape.
- Issue state: not physically present in this issue after the cover reference; he has already left for Circuit World.

## Shrink-Scope HQ Main Interior

Late-night common area inside Shrink-Scope's hidden HQ: couch, compact kitchen area, sleeping corridor doors, equipment lockers, repair bench, hidden reinforced door, emergency supplies, black surfaces with silver star details, polished wood panels with ruler-like black hash marks, crystal lens fixtures, microscope-inspired hardware, and no readable incidental text.

For this issue the HQ is badly damaged: cracked floor where Shrink-Scope's head hit, cracked wall behind Hoppette, laser scorch mark on the wall and Hoppette's suit, toppled furniture, smoke, dust, shattered panels, scattered debris, and the empty space where Bruiser and Machine Monarch were moments earlier.
