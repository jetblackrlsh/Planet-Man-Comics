# Treatment

## Logline

In the wreckage of Shrink-Scope's HQ, Shrink-Scope and Hoppette wake from Bruiser's attack and argue over the truth behind the Hoppers, until Planet-Man rises and turns their guilt into resolve.

## Central Conflict

Shrink-Scope needs someone to blame for Planet-Man's injuries. Hoppette needs to explain that her people's godhood crisis began long before she ever came for Planet-Man. Their conflict is not a fight of powers; it is a fight over responsibility after the room has already been destroyed.

## Theme

Shared failure can become shared duty only after the truth is spoken plainly.

## Ending

Planet-Man wakes battered but alive, places a steadying hand on Shrink-Scope's shoulder, and makes Bruiser's mercy into a future promise: "That is a mistake we will make sure he regrets."

## Emotional Arc

The issue begins in stunned silence and physical pain. Shrink-Scope moves from blurred panic to accusation. Hoppette moves from injury and fear to regretful honesty. By the end, Planet-Man's return does not erase the damage, but it gives the survivors a clear next direction.
