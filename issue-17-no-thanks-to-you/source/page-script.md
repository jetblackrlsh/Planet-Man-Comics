# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, late-night damaged HQ practical lighting, dust, smoke, cracked floor, cracked wall, laser scorch marks, bruises, pain, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is an adult divine humanoid with green vine dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten lava eyes when awake, and a fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct tiny miniature planet; in this issue he is battered and bruised, unconscious with eyes fully closed until the final page. Shrink-Scope is a heroic adult woman with polished wooden body covered in black ruler-like hash marks, long brown hair pulled back behind her head, glowing white crystal-ball eyes when awake, black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black starry miniskirt, glittery black heels, and a clear bruise on her forehead; she wakes first, groggy and angry. Hoppette is an adult humanoid curvy frog woman with smooth realistic amphibian skin, blue eyes, rounded frog-like face, long curly brown hair, frog-like hands and feet, and a futuristic flexible white leather hop suit with black accents; the suit has a dark scorch mark where Bruiser's laser hit her, and she clutches that injury while dizzy and in pain. Shrink-Scope HQ is a late-night damaged superhero base with couch, compact kitchen, sleeping corridor doors, equipment lockers, repair bench, black surfaces with silver stars, polished wood panels with ruler-like hash marks, crystal lens fixtures, cracked floor, cracked wall, toppled furniture, dust, smoke, shattered panels, and no readable incidental text.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo except intentional cover title text, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting. Do not use speaker-name prefixes. The quoted dialogue listed for each page must appear verbatim inside clean rectangular caption boxes.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: announce a quiet aftermath issue about blame, confession, and resolve.
Layout: cover image with one strong realistic-photo composition and modest poster clarity, still grounded in candid photo-comic style.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: NO THANKS TO YOU". Spell the brand name exactly as "PLANET-MAN" with one E in "PLANET"; never spell it "PLANEET-MAN". Small caption box: "After the strongest Hopper left, the truth stayed behind."
Visual beats: Damaged Shrink-Scope HQ at night, seen like a candid disaster photograph. Planet-Man lies unconscious on the cracked floor, eyes fully closed, battered and bruised. Shrink-Scope lies unconscious near a separate cracked floor impact with a bruise on her forehead. Hoppette sits unconscious against a cracked wall with a black scorch mark on her white-and-black hop suit. The empty damaged space implies Bruiser and Machine Monarch are gone.

## Page 02 First To Wake

Story role: Shrink-Scope wakes first, sees Hoppette, then finds Planet-Man.
Layout: six documentary panels in reading order, with the first panels slightly blurred from Shrink-Scope's point of view.
Caption boxes, in reading order, without visible numbers:
"The first thing Shrink-Scope saw was the room bending."
"Then the wall."
"Then Hoppette."
"Then Planet-Man."
"The blur did not matter anymore."
"She ran."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope's crystal eyes opening out of focus beside the cracked floor impact; blurred POV of damaged HQ and Hoppette slumped against the cracked wall; clearer panel of Hoppette unconscious with scorch mark on suit; Shrink-Scope turning and seeing Planet-Man unconscious and battered on the floor; Shrink-Scope stumbling upright with forehead bruise visible; Shrink-Scope rushing to Planet-Man and kneeling beside him to check whether he is alive.

## Page 03 Alive

Story role: Hoppette wakes, asks the unfinished question, and Shrink-Scope answers sharply.
Layout: six panels with two larger caption boxes for the exact dialogue.
Caption boxes, in reading order, without visible numbers:
"Hoppette woke to the taste of smoke."
"The room spun when she stood."
"Is he..?"
"He's alive. No thanks to you."
"Shrink-Scope never looked away from him."
"Hoppette held the burn and swallowed the answer."

## Page 04 Where Is Bruiser

Story role: Hoppette panics when she realizes Bruiser is gone, and Shrink-Scope identifies his attack.
Layout: six panels with large readable dialogue captions.
Caption boxes, in reading order, without visible numbers:

"I didn't even get to see the guy, but I recognize the feel of a frog tongue after our sparring session!"
"Shrink-Scope turned like the pain had found a target."
"Why did you bring him here?!"

"Her hand stayed over the wound."
Visual beats: Hoppette scanning the broken HQ and suddenly widening her blue eyes in panic; empty damaged floor where Bruiser should have been; Shrink-Scope angrily turning from Planet-Man toward Hoppette; Shrink-Scope yelling with bruise on forehead and wooden fist tense; Shrink-Scope pointing an accusatory finger at Hoppette; Hoppette taking a defensive step back while clutching her wound with one hand and raising the other hand as if to say hold on.

## Page 05 Different Paths

Story role: Hoppette begins the regretful explanation of why the hop suits exist.
Layout: five panels, slower and more intimate, with two wide panels built around long caption boxes.
Caption boxes, in reading order, without visible numbers:
"You and I both saw that our worlds were missing a god, but we both took different paths to fix that problem."
"Long before you brought Planet-Man here, I created the hop suits on my world."
"I've heard this before. You made the hop suits so that you could get Planet-Man to help other worlds than mine."
"Hoppette's regret hardened into annoyance."
"The broken room listened."
Visual beats: Hoppette lowering herself back against the cracked wall, face full of regret, scorch mark visible on her suit; close panel of Shrink-Scope looming in the foreground with Planet-Man still unconscious behind her; Hoppette explaining from the floor, one hand on the wound; Shrink-Scope interrupting with visible frustration; final wide panel showing the three survivors in the wrecked HQ, Hoppette seated, Shrink-Scope standing, Planet-Man still down.

## Page 06 Replacement Gods

Story role: Hoppette clarifies that the Hoppers created hop suits to replace Planet-Man, not merely find him.
Layout: six panels, using close expressions and large caption boxes.
Caption boxes, in reading order, without visible numbers:
"I only came for Planet-Man AFTER I knew he had returned. BEFORE I created them so that my people could fill-in as replacement gods."
"So the one who attacked us did so to try and replace Planet-Man?"
"Yes, because the Hoppers believe themselves to be the true gods and label Planet-Man as the abandoner."
"Shrink-Scope's anger bent into confusion."
"Hoppette nodded like the word still hurt."
"Abandoner."
Visual beats: Hoppette furrowing her brow with annoyance as she corrects Shrink-Scope; close panel of Shrink-Scope's crystal eyes widening with confusion and apprehension; Hoppette nodding solemnly, blue eyes low, still clutching the scorch injury; a small visual echo panel showing a white hop-suit seam glowing faintly near Hoppette's wrist with no readable labels; Shrink-Scope looking back toward Planet-Man, processing the accusation of abandoner; final tight panel of Hoppette's defeated expression.

## Page 07 Blasphemer

Story role: Hoppette admits that she was cast out and underestimated Bruiser.
Layout: six panels, tense vertical compositions around Shrink-Scope looming over Hoppette.
Caption boxes, in reading order, without visible numbers:
"So all your talk of wanting Planet-Man to save other worlds.."
"Was all true. I was once the greatest mind of my kind. Now I am labeled a blasphemer, and I am constantly on the run. I thought the others would be too afraid to face the abandoner head on."
"But you were wrong."
"Bruiser is the strongest of the Hoppers. I don't know why he didn't kill us all."
"Hoppette looked down."
"Shrink-Scope's fist stayed closed."
Visual beats: Shrink-Scope stomping over to Hoppette and looming over her, accusatory and bruised; Hoppette looking up with serious exhausted honesty; Hoppette interrupting with the long confession, framed by cracked wall and scorch mark; Shrink-Scope balling her fist and growling through anger; Hoppette lowering her eyes in defeat as she admits Bruiser's strength; final panel of Planet-Man's fingers subtly moving in the background while the two women do not notice yet.

## Page 08 Mistake

Caption boxes, in reading order, without visible numbers:
"The floor shifted behind them."
"Planet-Man stood before either of them could turn."
"He was battered."
"He was awake."
"That is a mistake we will make sure he regrets."
"Shrink-Scope went still under his hand."
"Hoppette finally breathed."
Visual beats: Planet-Man's molten eyes opening faintly while he is still bruised on the floor; his black rocky hand pressing against cracked flooring as he rises; Shrink-Scope and Hoppette turning in surprise; Planet-Man standing battered and bruised but conscious; Planet-Man placing a calming hand on Shrink-Scope's shoulder; Shrink-Scope's anger freezing into relief; final panel shows Planet-Man, Shrink-Scope, and Hoppette in the damaged HQ, still wounded but emotionally aligned toward the next fight.
