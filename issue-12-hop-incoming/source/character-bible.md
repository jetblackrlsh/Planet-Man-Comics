# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The pages should feel like documentary phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels, even when the action becomes superpowered. Use believable camera perspective, physical textures, natural imperfect framing, practical campfire and moonlight, photoreal VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes. No anime, no painted illustration, no cartoon rendering, no comic-book linework, no studio-poster polish except modest cover clarity.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays.

## Hoppette

- Role: runaway inventor and protagonist, hunted by Bruiser.
- Body: adult humanoid curvy frog woman, athletic and agile, with smooth olive-green realistic amphibian skin details and frog-like hands and feet.
- Face and eyes: expressive rounded frog-like face, large blue eyes, wide mouth, anxious tenderness that can harden into defiance.
- Hair: long curly brown hair that catches firelight and flies outward during leaps.
- Outfit: futuristic flexible leather white bodysuit with black accents, fitted practical panels, subtle travel-device seams, no readable labels, no logos.
- Powers and gear: massive frog tongue, superhuman leaping, tree-branch landings, tongue-swinging, and travel between miniature planets through glowing white lily pad glyphs produced by her suit. Red holographic warning displays can appear near her suit as tracking alerts.
- Expression range: exhausted solitude, surprised warmth, alarmed calculation, guilty concern, fierce argument, desperate focus.
- Silhouette: spring-loaded crouch, long curls, white-and-black bodysuit, frog-like hands and feet, luminous lily pad glyph motif.

## Stray Dog

- Role: innocent runaway who mirrors Hoppette's situation.
- Body: medium-sized scruffy mixed-breed dog, realistic tan-and-white coat, floppy ears, dark nose, expressive brown eyes, thin frame without looking neglected.
- Personality in visuals: cautious curiosity at the campfire, startled barking at Bruiser, frightened whimpering when trapped in mucus.
- Continuity: no collar, no readable tags, same tan-and-white coat and floppy ears on every page.

## Bruiser

- Role: antagonist and hunter from Hoppette's home world.
- Body: muscular adult humanoid frog man, much broader and heavier than Hoppette, green amphibian skin with darker mottling, thick neck, powerful legs and hands.
- Face and eyes: stern frog-like face, black eye-patch over one eye at first; beneath it is a ruby red cybernetic laser eye with mechanical lens rings and glowing red core.
- Outfit: futuristic white leather bodysuit with black accents, heavier and more armored than Hoppette's, black armored shoulder pads, black utility seams, no readable labels, no logos.
- Powers and gear: giant frog tongue that can strike like a whip, clear adhesive mucus netting, extreme leaping, white lily pad glyph arrival, ruby cybernetic laser blasts from eye.
- Expression range: cold mission focus, cruel smile, religious certainty, amused taunting, frustrated rage.
- Silhouette: bulky frog-warrior frame, black eye-patch, black armored shoulder pads, white-and-black suit, red laser eye when revealed.

## Miniature-Planet Forest

- Location: a random forest on an unknown miniature planet, not Shrink-Scope's HQ and not Hoppette's home world.
- Visual language: towering trees, thick branches, moss, ferns, small surreal hints of a curved tiny-world horizon between trunks, unfamiliar oversized leaves, warm campfire light, cool moonlight, and photoreal white lily pad glyphs glowing on soil.
- Continuity details: Hoppette starts beside a small campfire, hides in branches above it, then flees through connected treetops into a moonlit clearing at the end of the treeline. Bruiser's laser cuts branches and leaves smoke-scorched wood.
