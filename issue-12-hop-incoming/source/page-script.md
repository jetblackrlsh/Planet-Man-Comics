# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, believable forest firelight and moonlight, real bark, moss, smoke, wet amphibian skin, photoreal action VFX, realistic motion blur, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Hoppette is an adult humanoid curvy frog woman with smooth olive-green realistic amphibian skin, large blue eyes, expressive rounded frog-like face, long curly brown hair, frog-like hands and feet, athletic spring-loaded posture, and a futuristic flexible leather white bodysuit with black accents, fitted practical panels, subtle travel-device seams, no readable labels, no logos. The stray dog is a medium-sized scruffy tan-and-white mixed-breed dog with floppy ears, expressive brown eyes, no collar, and a cautious but gentle face. Bruiser is a muscular adult humanoid frog man, much broader and heavier than Hoppette, with green mottled amphibian skin, a stern frog-like face, black eye-patch, futuristic white leather bodysuit with black accents, black armored shoulder pads, and a hidden ruby red cybernetic laser eye beneath the eye-patch; no readable labels, no logos.

Setting continuity: random miniature-planet forest at night, towering trees, moss, ferns, occasional impossible curved-horizon glimpses between trunks, warm campfire light early, cool moonlight in treetops and clearing, glowing white lily pad glyph portals on soil, red holographic hop warning, wet clear adhesive mucus, smoke-scorched branches from ruby laser fire.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, warning text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting. Do not use speaker-name prefixes.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: announce Hoppette as hunted and establish the forest chase threat.
Layout: cover image with one strong realistic-photo composition and modest poster clarity, still grounded in the candid photo-comic style.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: HOP INCOMING". Small caption box: "Hoppette runs from home."
Visual beats: Hoppette crouches high on a moonlit tree branch, blue eyes worried, long curly hair tangled with leaves, white-and-black suit catching glyph light. Far below, Bruiser stands in the forest clearing with black eye-patch raised to reveal a ruby laser eye, smoke from a cut branch, and a glowing white lily pad glyph under his boots. The stray dog is visible near the campfire behind a clear mucus net.
