# Treatment

## Title

Planet-Man: Shock Step

## Logline

After burning himself out making specialized golem guardians for the worlds he once harmed, Planet-Man passes out during a chase and has to admit to Shrink-Scope that heroism has become another form of obsession.

## Central Conflict

Planet-Man wants to protect every world personally by building perfect guardians for each one, but the work is consuming the rest he needs to function. Shock Step's robbery turns that private exhaustion into a public failure.

## Theme

Repair cannot become another compulsion. Responsibility needs balance, or it recreates the harm it is trying to fix.

## Emotional Arc

Planet-Man begins the issue pushing through exhaustion because the backlog feels morally urgent. After collapsing, he wakes embarrassed and defensive. Shrink-Scope helps him name the pattern: his old rush to create miniature planets has returned under a more heroic excuse. He ends the issue accepting a small nightly ritual that gives him a practical way to stop.

## Ending

The final page resolves the emotional conflict visually: Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope sit together in his bedroom instead of returning to the workshop, both yawning as the unfinished miniature worlds wait in the background.

## Cast

- Planet-Man: exhausted creator-hero, normal adult size in Shrink-Scope's world.
- Shrink-Scope: worried partner and grounded voice of balance.
- Shock Step: speedster supervillain made from blue lightning, copper wire hair, and battery eyes.
- Golem Guardians: two humanoid protectors made from miniature planets; one rescues Planet-Man while the other continues the chase.
