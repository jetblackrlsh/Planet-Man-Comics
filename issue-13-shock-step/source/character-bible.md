# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The pages should feel like documentary phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels, even when the action becomes superpowered. Use believable camera perspective, physical textures, natural imperfect framing, practical city and bedroom lighting, photoreal lightning and flight VFX, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays. No speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no speaker-name prefixes, no subtitles, no watermarks, and no extra decorative text.

## Planet-Man

- Role: creator-hero trying to protect many miniature worlds at once.
- Body: adult divine humanoid figure, normal human scale in Shrink-Scope's world, black rocky volcanic skin with grounded stone texture.
- Face and eyes: molten lava eyes that glow orange through fatigue; eyelids heavy or closed during the collapse.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, organic and physically textured.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct tiny miniature planet; no readable labels or logos.
- Powers and props: flight, telekinetic control over miniature planets, world-shaping tools in his bedroom workshop.
- Issue state: visibly sleep-deprived, drooping posture, drifting flight path, ash dust and rooftop grit after the crash, emotionally embarrassed but honest by the end.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: Planet-Man's partner and the issue's voice of balance.
- Body: heroic adult woman with polished wooden body covered in black ruler-like hash marks.
- Face and eyes: glowing white crystal-ball eyes; expression range from worried and disturbed to tender and sleepy.
- Outfit: black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, glittery black heels; no readable labels or logos.
- Powers and motifs: size-changing hero, crystal optics, microscope-inspired details, black and silver star visual language.

## Shock Step

- Role: speedster supervillain who exposes Planet-Man's exhaustion.
- Body: adult humanoid male silhouette made entirely from branching blue lightning and electric plasma, with no normal skin.
- Face and eyes: two large round flat battery discs in place of eyes, metallic rims, simple expressive electric mouth shape.
- Hair: wild copper wires forming hair, whipping backward as he runs.
- Outfit and props: no fabric costume; carries a dark stolen-jewel bag with gems spilling flashes of color; no readable logos.
- Movement: ground-level sprinting with blue electric trails, sparks, heat shimmer, cracked asphalt, and reflected lightning on city windows.
- Expression range: gleeful speed, taunting confidence, delighted surprise when Planet-Man crashes, alarm when a golem guardian continues the chase.

## Golem Guardians

- Role: Planet-Man's created protectors, proof that his good idea has become too consuming.
- Body: adult bulky humanoid guardians made from many realistic miniature planets and planetoids arranged into clear anatomy: head, torso, arms, hands, legs, and feet.
- Details: oceans, forests, deserts, storm bands, volcanic cracks, city lights, cratered moons, and ringed gas giants visible across their bodies; pale root systems, gravity filaments, and vine-like connective tissues bind the planets together.
- Face and eyes: calm blue-white glowing eyes formed in rocky planet faces.
- Behavior: one kneels gently to recover Planet-Man; one launches into the street chase with calm purpose.

## City Chase Area

- Dense threatened city streets at night, wet asphalt, jewelry store exterior with broken glass but no readable signage, emergency lighting, high rooftops, alley steam, reflections, and handheld-photo urgency.

## Planet-Man HQ Bedroom

- Human-scale bedroom inside Shrink-Scope's hidden HQ. Includes bed, shelves, desk/workbench, containment rings, unfinished miniature worlds, black volcanic stone textures, green vine-like cables, molten orange accent lights, and practical tools for shaping miniature planets. It should feel like a real living space being overtaken by work.
