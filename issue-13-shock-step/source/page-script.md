# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, believable night-city lighting, realistic bedroom practical lighting, photoreal blue lightning VFX, grounded crash dust and rooftop grit, miniature-planet surfaces, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is an adult divine humanoid normal human scale in Shrink-Scope's world, with green vine dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten orange lava eyes, and a fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct tiny miniature planet; in this issue he is visibly exhausted, eyelids heavy, posture sagging, and later dusty from a rooftop crash. Shrink-Scope is a heroic adult woman with glowing white crystal-ball eyes, a polished wooden body covered in black ruler-like hash marks, black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, and glittery black heels; her expression is worried, disturbed, then tender. Shock Step is an adult humanoid male speedster made entirely from branching blue lightning and electric plasma, with wild copper wires for hair, large round flat battery discs for eyes, and a dark stolen-jewel bag with colored gems; no normal skin, no fabric costume. The golem guardians are bulky adult humanoids made from many realistic miniature planets and planetoids connected by pale root systems and gravity filaments, with calm blue-white eyes.

Setting continuity: dense threatened city streets at night with wet asphalt, broken jewelry store glass but no readable signage, emergency light reflections, rooftops, alley steam, and handheld chase energy. Planet-Man's HQ bedroom is human-scale, practical, and cluttered with shelves, containment rings, workbench tools, unfinished miniature worlds, black volcanic stone textures, green vine-like cables, and molten orange accent lights.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo except the intentional cover title text, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting. Do not use speaker-name prefixes.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: announce the chase, Shock Step's identity, and Planet-Man's dangerous exhaustion.
Layout: cover image with one strong realistic-photo composition and modest poster clarity, still grounded in candid photo-comic style.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: SHOCK STEP". Small caption box: "He was too tired to fly."
Visual beats: Planet-Man flying low and unstable over a wet night city street, eyelids nearly closed, one hand trailing down as if losing altitude. Below him, Shock Step sprints away from a broken jewelry store with a bag of stolen jewels, his body made of blue lightning, copper wire hair streaming back, battery eyes gleaming. Blue electric trails reflect on asphalt and windows.

"No workshops. No backlog."

"Good night."

Visual beats: Planet-Man admitting the itch to create, seated on the bed; Shrink-Scope taking his hand; the workbench lights dimming while miniature worlds remain safely contained; they sit shoulder to shoulder talking quietly; Planet-Man hugs Shrink-Scope; both yawn at the same time; final panel shows them calm and sleepy in the warm room, with unfinished worlds in the background but no one reaching for them.
