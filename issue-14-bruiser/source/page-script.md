# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, late-night HQ practical lighting, photoreal white lily pad glyph light, tongue-grapple motion, ruby laser VFX, miniature-planet surfaces, smoke, impact dust, flame, ice reflection, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Planet-Man is an adult divine humanoid normal human scale in Shrink-Scope's world, with green vine dreadlocks, black rocky volcanic skin, molten orange lava eyes, and a fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct tiny miniature planet; in this issue he is visibly exhausted with heavy eyelids, then enraged after Shrink-Scope is hurt, and his right fist becomes painful after Bruiser crushes it. Shrink-Scope is a heroic adult woman with glowing white crystal-ball eyes, a polished wooden body covered in black ruler-like hash marks, black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, and glittery black heels; she is sleepy, annoyed, concerned, then unconscious after the ambush. Hoppette is an adult humanoid curvy frog woman with smooth realistic amphibian skin, blue eyes, expressive rounded frog-like face, long curly brown hair, frog-like hands and feet, athletic spring-loaded posture, and a futuristic flexible white leather bodysuit with black accents; she arrives sweating and frightened, then fights and is knocked unconscious. Bruiser is a massive muscular adult humanoid frog man, broader and heavier than Hoppette, with green mottled amphibian skin, thick neck, powerful frog legs, large frog hands and feet, stern frog-like face, black eye patch, heavy futuristic white leather bodysuit with black accents and black armored shoulders, giant frog tongue, and a ruby red cybernetic laser eye under the eye patch.

Setting continuity: Shrink-Scope HQ main common area late at night, with couch, compact kitchen area, sleeping corridor doors, equipment lockers, repair bench, hidden reinforced door, emergency supplies, black surfaces with silver star details, polished wood panels with ruler-like hash marks, crystal lens fixtures, microscope-inspired hardware, and no readable incidental text. The room becomes progressively damaged by impacts, laser scorch marks, shattered wall panels, smoke, dust, and scattered furniture.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo except the intentional cover title text, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting. Do not use speaker-name prefixes.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: announce the late-night home invasion and Planet-Man's exhausted rage.
Layout: cover image with one strong realistic-photo composition and modest poster clarity, still grounded in candid photo-comic style.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: BRUISER". Small caption box: "He was too tired for mercy."
Visual beats: Planet-Man stands in the damaged HQ common room with heavy eyelids and blazing molten eyes, one injured fist clenched, miniature planets orbiting unevenly. Bruiser looms opposite him with his eye patch lifted and shattered ruby cybernetic eye glowing, white bodysuit armored and threatening. Shrink-Scope and Hoppette are visible unconscious in the background, white lily pad glyph light fading on the floor, smoke and wall damage around them.
