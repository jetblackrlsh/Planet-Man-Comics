# Treatment

## Title

Planet-Man: Bruiser

## Logline

When Hoppette bursts into Shrink-Scope's HQ with a warning, Bruiser follows her through the same lily pad gateway and forces an exhausted Planet-Man into a brutal close-quarters fight he can only win by turning anger into precision.

## Central Conflict

Planet-Man needs rest, and Shrink-Scope is trying to protect him from another late-night crisis. Bruiser turns that care into a liability by attacking Shrink-Scope first, forcing Planet-Man to fight while depleted, furious, and nearly outmatched.

## Theme

Protection is not the same as control. Shrink-Scope tries to take the burden from Planet-Man; Planet-Man still has to answer when the threat reaches their home.

## Emotional Arc

Planet-Man begins half-asleep and ready to obey Shrink-Scope's bedtime boundary. When Bruiser hurts Shrink-Scope and Hoppette, exhaustion gives way to rage. The fight tests whether rage alone can carry him. He wins only when he stops throwing power blindly and uses the exact right mini-planets at the exact right moments. The victory leaves him upright only long enough to see Bruiser fall before his own body finally shuts down.

## Ending

The final page resolves the fight visually: Bruiser lies unconscious against the damaged HQ wall, Shrink-Scope and Hoppette are still down, and Planet-Man collapses from exhaustion after saving them.

## Cast

- Planet-Man: exhausted creator-hero, normal adult size in Shrink-Scope's world.
- Shrink-Scope: Planet-Man's partner, trying to keep him from burning himself out.
- Hoppette: anxious frog-woman ally using white lily pad glyph travel.
- Bruiser: massive frog-man attacker with a tongue grapple and cybernetic laser eye.
- Golem guardians: mentioned as insufficient but not active in the fight.

## Story Structure

- Cover: exhausted Planet-Man faces Bruiser in the broken HQ common room.
- Page 2: late-night couch scene and Hoppette's sudden arrival.
- Page 3: Shrink-Scope tries to send Planet-Man to bed, then Bruiser ambushes her.
- Page 4: Planet-Man and Hoppette attack, but Bruiser overpowers both.
- Page 5: Planet-Man blinds Bruiser with a gas planet and bombards him with rocky mini-planets.
- Page 6: Bruiser tackles Planet-Man and prepares a killing laser; Planet-Man reflects it with an ice planet.
- Page 7: Planet-Man follows with ringed mini-planets as cutting weapons.
- Page 8: Planet-Man finishes with a volcanic mini-planet blast, then collapses.
