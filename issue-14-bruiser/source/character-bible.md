# Character And Reference Bible

## Shared Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic pages. The pages should feel like documentary phone photos or handheld photojournalism arranged into comic panels, even when the action becomes superpowered. Use believable camera perspective, physical textures, natural imperfect framing, late-night practical HQ lighting, photoreal glyph light, tongue-grapple motion, laser VFX, smoke, impact dust, miniature-planet surfaces, flame, ice reflection, and clean rectangular caption boxes.

All readable story text belongs inside generated rectangular caption boxes or cover title treatment inside the image art. No later text overlays. No speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no speaker-name prefixes, no subtitles, no watermarks, no extra decorative text, and no blank caption boxes.

## Planet-Man

- Role: exhausted creator-hero forced into a home invasion fight.
- Body: adult divine humanoid figure, normal human scale in Shrink-Scope's world, black rocky volcanic skin with grounded stone texture.
- Face and eyes: molten lava eyes that glow orange; heavy eyelids from exhaustion, then visibly enraged after Shrink-Scope is knocked unconscious.
- Hair: green vine dreadlocks, organic and physically textured.
- Outfit: fitted beaded bodysuit where every bead is a distinct tiny miniature planet; no readable labels or logos.
- Powers and props: telekinetic miniature planets, including gaseous, rocky, reflective ice, ringed blade, and volcanic flame mini-planets.
- Issue state: exhausted throughout; pained right fist after Bruiser crushes it; dusty, bruised, and increasingly drained by the final page.

## Shrink-Scope

- Role: Planet-Man's partner and protective mission controller.
- Body: heroic adult woman with polished wooden body covered in black ruler-like hash marks.
- Face and eyes: glowing white crystal-ball eyes; expression mixes annoyance, concern, discipline, and alarm before being knocked unconscious.
- Outfit: black crop top with crystal microscope insignia, black miniskirt covered in silver stars, glittery black heels; no readable labels or logos.
- Powers and motifs: clairvoyance, size-changing hero, crystal optics, microscope-inspired details, black and silver star visual language.
- Issue state: relaxed and sleepy at first; unconscious after Bruiser slams her head into the floor.

## Hoppette

- Role: urgent messenger and outmatched ally.
- Body: adult humanoid curvy frog woman with smooth realistic amphibian skin, blue eyes, expressive rounded frog-like face, long curly brown hair, frog-like hands and feet, and athletic spring-loaded posture.
- Outfit: futuristic flexible leather white bodysuit with black accents, fitted practical panels, subtle travel-device seams, no readable labels, no logos.
- Powers and motifs: white lily pad glyph travel; fast leaping tackles.
- Issue state: arrives in a cold sweat and panic; later knocked unconscious into the back wall by Bruiser's laser.

## Bruiser

- Role: overwhelming frog-man invader and physical antagonist.
- Body: muscular adult humanoid frog man, much broader and heavier than Hoppette, green mottled realistic amphibian skin, thick neck, powerful frog legs, large frog hands and feet, stern frog-like face.
- Outfit: black eye patch over one eye, futuristic white leather bodysuit with black accents, heavier and more armored than Hoppette's, black armored shoulder pads, black utility seams, no readable labels, no logos.
- Weapons: giant frog tongue used as a constricting whip; ruby red cybernetic laser eye hidden under the eye patch.
- Issue state: starts imposing and almost untouched; by page 7 his bodysuit is tattered, his cybernetic eye is shattered, and he is wounded but still standing.

## Shrink-Scope HQ Main Interior

Late-night common area inside Shrink-Scope's hidden HQ: compact kitchen area, couch, sleeping corridor doors, equipment lockers, repair bench, hidden reinforced door, emergency supplies, black surfaces with silver star details, polished wood panels with ruler-like black hash marks, crystal lens fixtures, microscope-inspired hardware, clean practical organization, and no readable incidental text.

## White Lily Pad Glyph

Photoreal floor magic/technology effect: a glowing white lily pad symbol on the HQ floor, bright enough to cast cold light onto feet, couch legs, and polished surfaces. It should feel like physically present light, not a flat graphic overlay.
