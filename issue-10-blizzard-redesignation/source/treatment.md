# Treatment

## Logline

After fire-villain Char Cole delivers the defeated ice-planet golem Blizzard to Doctor Metal Fist's lab, the villains' argument over the nature of Planet-Man's Golem Guardians gives Hoppette the opening she needs to rescue Blizzard and send him to a different world.

## Central Conflict

Blizzard is helpless in enemy custody, weakened by fire and trapped inside Doctor Metal Fist's scanner. Char Cole and Doctor Metal Fist want answers about the new heroes, but their own arrogance turns the interrogation into a fight. Hoppette uses that distraction to rescue Blizzard, then reveals that the rescue is also a reassignment.

## Theme

Created life still deserves agency and protection, but responsibility can require serving where the need is greatest rather than where the first battle began.

## Ending

Hoppette leaves Blizzard on a different world under Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope's agreement. Blizzard is still battered, but he sees a cold horizon, hears that this world's villains do not use fire, and accepts the first step of becoming its protector.

## Emotional Arc

Blizzard begins defeated, overheated, and treated like a specimen. The villains' insults make clear that even enemies recognize the Golem Guardians are alive. Hoppette's rescue restores Blizzard's agency, but not comfort: he learns that rescue and duty arrived together. The final beat turns his exhaustion into wary resolve.

## Cast

- **Blizzard:** A living Golem Guardian made from miniature ice planets, connected by pale frosted root systems. He is battered, bruised, melting at the edges, and covered in condensation after losing to Char Cole.
- **Char Cole:** A fire-powered supervillain whose entire body is living flame, except for charcoal-black eyes. He is proud, volatile, and easily insulted.
- **Doctor Metal Fist:** A mad scientist with tan skin, brown eyes, bald head, round black glasses, silver robotic arms, blue dress shirt, white lab coat, brown slacks, and black shoes. He is brilliant, cruel, and contemptuous.
- **Hoppette:** An adult humanoid frog woman with blue eyes, long curly brown hair, smooth amphibian skin, frog-like hands and feet, and a futuristic flexible white leather bodysuit with black accents. She travels through glowing white lily pad glyphs.
