# Character Bible

## Blizzard

- Role: captured Golem Guardian and reassigned protector.
- Body: adult humanoid golem made from many realistic miniature ice planets, planetoids, frozen moons, snowfields, cracked glaciers, frozen oceans, white cloud bands, and pale blue atmospheres.
- Connective anatomy: complex pale frosted root systems run between all the mini-planets that make up his body, visibly acting like a nervous system and muscle system.
- Condition: battered and bruised throughout the lab sequence; cracked ice, impact marks, slumped posture, heavy condensation, meltwater dripping from the body, fogging around the scanning pod.
- Eyes and expression: dim cold blue glow inside an icy head cluster; exhausted, pained, wary, then grateful and reluctantly determined.
- Silhouette: bulky humanoid made of clustered ice worlds, with visible root strands and cold mist. He should not look like a normal person in costume.

## Char Cole

- Role: fire-powered supervillain who defeated Blizzard and brought him to Doctor Metal Fist.
- Body: entire adult humanoid body is made of living flames, with a human-like muscular silhouette formed from orange, yellow, and red fire.
- Eyes: solid charcoal-black eyes embedded in the flames; no normal skin, hair, clothing, or accessories.
- Power state: usual flames are orange and yellow; when angered, his whole body glows harsh red and emits more sparks and heat distortion.
- Expression range: victorious swagger, confusion, impressed curiosity, wounded pride, open rage.
- Silhouette: walking human-shaped bonfire with charcoal eyes and heat shimmer.

## Doctor Metal Fist

- Role: mad scientist analyzing Blizzard and provoking Char Cole.
- Body and face: adult man with tan skin, brown eyes, bald head, round black glasses, severe facial expression.
- Arms: two silver robotic arms with articulated chrome fingers, reinforced knuckles, and visible mechanical joints; they can type rapidly and deflect fireballs.
- Outfit: blue dress shirt under a white lab coat, brown slacks, black shoes.
- Expression range: smug focus, contempt, sudden anger, cold tactical control.
- Silhouette: bald scientist in lab coat with round glasses and oversized silver mechanical arms.

## Hoppette

- Role: rescuer and envoy for Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope's world-protection deal.
- Body: adult humanoid curvy frog woman with smooth realistic amphibian skin details, blue eyes, expressive rounded frog-like face, frog-like hands and feet.
- Hair: long curly brown hair.
- Outfit: futuristic flexible leather white bodysuit with black accents, fitted practical panels, subtle travel-device seams, no readable labels and no logos.
- Powers: superhuman hops and travel between miniature worlds through glowing white lily pad glyphs.
- Expression range: alert stealth, protective urgency, dry practical confidence, encouraging seriousness.
- Silhouette: spring-loaded crouch, long curls, white-and-black bodysuit, luminous lily pad glyph motif.

## Doctor Metal Fist's Lab

- Location: futuristic mad-scientist lab, clean but threatening, with metal flooring, cold blue-white scan lights, black glass computer terminals, robotic armatures, cables, diagnostic monitors with abstract unreadable graphics, and an open cylindrical scanning pod.
- Central prop: open scanning pod large enough to hold Blizzard, with transparent curved panels, sensor rings, cold vapor, and bright analysis beams.
- Continuity: the white lily pad glyph forms on the lab floor beside the scanning pod during the villain fight.

## New World

- Location: an alien city or frontier world that needs protection but is not dominated by fire-powered villains. It should feel cold, wet, or twilight-blue enough for Blizzard to plausibly recover.
- Visual motif: white lily pad glyph on the ground, distant threatened buildings or landscape, no readable signs, and a clear sense that Blizzard has arrived somewhere new.

## Text And Continuity Rules

- Use caption boxes only for narration and rare short speech.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating subtitles, watermarks, logos, or extra unreadable decorative text.
- All readable story text must be generated directly inside the comic page art.
- Do not leave blank caption boxes, blank pages, or art-only panels for later typesetting.
- Keep Blizzard, Char Cole, Doctor Metal Fist, and Hoppette visually consistent across pages.
