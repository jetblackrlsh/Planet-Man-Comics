# Page Script And Generation Prompts

## Common Prompt Block

Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Create a complete realistic photo-comic page, not a single image, not anime, not painted illustration. Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism panels with natural imperfect framing, believable lab and alien-world lighting, grounded physical materials, photoreal fire, ice, condensation, robotics, scanning beams, and portal VFX, plus clean rectangular caption boxes.

Character continuity: Blizzard is a living adult humanoid Golem Guardian made from many realistic miniature ice planets, frozen moons, snowfields, cracked glaciers, frozen oceans, white cloud bands, pale blue atmospheres, and pale frosted root systems connecting all the mini-planets like a pseudo nervous system and muscle system; in the lab he is battered, bruised, cracked, slumped, dripping condensation and meltwater, with dim cold blue eyes. Char Cole is an adult humanoid supervillain whose entire body is living orange-yellow flame except for solid charcoal-black eyes; when angered, his flames glow harsh red, with sparks and heat distortion. Doctor Metal Fist is a tan-skinned adult mad scientist with brown eyes, bald head, round black glasses, two silver robotic arms with articulated chrome fingers and reinforced knuckles, a blue dress shirt, white lab coat, brown slacks, and black shoes. Hoppette is an adult humanoid curvy frog woman with blue eyes, long curly brown hair, smooth realistic amphibian skin details, frog-like hands and feet, and a futuristic flexible leather white bodysuit with black accents and subtle travel-device seams. Doctor Metal Fist's lab is a futuristic mad-scientist lab with metal flooring, cold blue-white scan lights, black glass computer terminals, robotic armatures, cables, abstract unreadable diagnostics, and an open cylindrical scanning pod.

Text rule: caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no extra unreadable decorative text. All readable story text, title text, and caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes or art-only panels for later typesetting.

## Page 01 Cover

Story role: present the capture and villain-lab premise immediately.
Layout: cover image with one dramatic candid-photo lab composition, with title text integrated into the generated page art.
Readable text inside generated cover art: Title text: "PLANET-MAN: BLIZZARD". Small caption box: "A guardian falls. A world still waits."
Visual beats: Char Cole strides into Doctor Metal Fist's cold futuristic lab carrying the limp battered Blizzard over one fiery shoulder. Blizzard's ice planets are cracked and wet with condensation. Doctor Metal Fist stands beside an open scanning pod, silver robotic arms ready. Firelight and blue scanner light clash across the lab.

## Page 02 Into The Scanner

Story role: establish that Char Cole has already beaten Blizzard and is delivering him for analysis.
Layout: six documentary panels in reading order.
Caption boxes:
1. "Char Cole brought proof of victory."
2. "Blizzard had fought until the frost gave out."
3. "Doctor. I brought your specimen."
4. "Doctor Metal Fist opened the pod."
5. "The ice guardian could barely lift his head."
6. "Then the scan began."
Visual beats: Char Cole entering the lab with limp Blizzard; close-up of Blizzard's cracked ice planets and condensation; Doctor Metal Fist beside open pod; Char Cole placing Blizzard into the pod; Blizzard weakly gripping the rim; scanner rings lighting blue around him.

## Page 03 Spawn Or System

Story role: reveal that Doctor Metal Fist is analyzing the origin and biology of the Golem Guardians.
Layout: six panels with terminal work, scan projections, and tense villain dialogue.
Caption boxes:
1. "Metrics poured out in cold light."
2. "Where are these heroes coming from?"
3. "Spawn of dreadful Planet-Man."
4. "Planet-Man has kids?"
5. "Created. Not birthed."
6. "The scanner saw what anger missed."
Visual beats: Doctor Metal Fist typing rapidly with silver robotic fingers; holographic abstract diagrams of Blizzard's body with no readable technical labels; Char Cole leaning in with charcoal eyes; Doctor sneering at him; scan beams showing mini ice planets connected by pale roots; Blizzard trapped and silent in pod.

## Page 04 Alive Enough To Insult

Story role: explain the golems' root systems and escalate Doctor Metal Fist's contempt toward Char Cole.
Layout: six panels, colder and more intimate around the scanning pod.
Caption boxes:
1. "Each world was not alone."
2. "Roots joined planet to planet."
3. "Nerves. Muscles. A system."
4. "So they are alive?"
5. "Obviously, nitwit."
6. "Fire took the insult personally."
Visual beats: macro panel of pale frosted roots spanning between Blizzard's mini-planets; scanner projection indicating root-system motion without readable labels; Char Cole looking impressed and confused; Doctor Metal Fist shouting; Char Cole's flames shifting from orange to harsh red; Blizzard's dim eyes registering the word alive.

## Page 05 Heat Against Metal

Story role: turn the villains' bickering into a physical fight.
Layout: six kinetic action panels with one larger fireball-deflection panel.
Caption boxes:
1. "Respect me."
2. "Doctor Metal Fist answered with contempt."
3. "The lab became an argument."
4. "Char Cole threw fire."
5. "Metal Fist threw it back."
6. "No one watched the floor."
Visual beats: Char Cole looming red-hot over Doctor Metal Fist; Doctor pointing one silver robotic finger in Char's face; fireball launching across the lab; Doctor crossing robotic arms to deflect it; sparks and reflected flame washing across the scanner pod; close-up of empty floor beside the pod beginning to glow faint white.

## Page 06 The Quiet Hop

Story role: show Hoppette rescuing Blizzard while the villains are distracted.
Layout: seven panels with stealthy timing and portal movement.
Caption boxes:
1. "A white lily pad opened beside the scanner."
2. "Hoppette came through low and quiet."
3. "The villains kept swinging."
4. "Blizzard was colder than hope."
5. "Stay with me."
6. "One lift. One jump."
7. "The lab lost its prisoner."
Visual beats: white lily pad glyph forming on lab floor; Hoppette crouching out of the glyph unnoticed; Char Cole and Doctor Metal Fist fighting in the background; Hoppette opening or reaching into the scanner pod; Hoppette supporting weakened Blizzard; both dropping into the glowing white glyph as fire and robotic arms clash behind them.

## Page 07 Redesignated

Story role: move Blizzard to another world and reveal Hoppette's deal with Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope.
Layout: six calmer panels on a cold alien world.
Caption boxes:
1. "Another world received them."
2. "Thank you."
3. "Not yet."
4. "Where are we?"
5. "Your new assignment."
6. "Rescue had brought orders with it."
Visual beats: Hoppette and Blizzard hopping out of a white lily pad glyph on a cold twilight alien street or frontier platform; Blizzard collapsed on one knee, still cracked and wet; Hoppette helping him upright; distant threatened skyline or strange landscape; Blizzard looking confused; Hoppette pointing toward the world he must protect.

## Page 08 A World Without Fire

Story role: resolve the issue with Blizzard accepting his new protected world and Hoppette leaving.
Layout: seven panels with a final wide hopeful image.
Caption boxes:
1. "Who authorized this?"
2. "Planet-Man and Shrink-Scope."
3. "She helped them."
4. "They sent guardians where worlds needed hands."
5. "No fire villains here."
6. "Blizzard stood a little taller."
7. "Good luck."
Visual beats: Hoppette explaining the deal while Blizzard looks wary; do not show Planet-Man or Shrink-Scope physically, in the clouds, as holograms, or as off-model figures; panel 2 should focus on Hoppette naming them in a caption while Blizzard listens; Blizzard looking over the cold new world; his condensation slowing as frost returns; Hoppette smiling, practical and sincere; Hoppette hopping into a white lily pad glyph; final wide panel shows Blizzard alone but upright, facing the new world he will protect.
