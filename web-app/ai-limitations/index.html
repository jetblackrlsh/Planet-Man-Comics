Creative Process

AI Limitations

Planet-Man Comics uses AI as part of the process for creating its comics and stories. The site is open about that instead of trying to make the work look like it came from a fully traditional production pipeline.

Read the comics

The rough edges are part of the archive.

AI can introduce visual inconsistencies, panel-to-panel mistakes, odd details, unclear moments, and story beats that do not always land as cleanly as intended.

Visible Mistakes Stay Visible

The comics and stories on this app do not try to hide that they are created with AI help. When AI leaves behind mismatched costumes, changing faces, strange props, unclear staging, or rough story clarity, those artifacts are usually retained as part of the finished page.

Read Them For What They Are

The invitation is to accept the AI nature of these comics and appreciate them on those terms. They are experimental superhero stories made with new tools, and that includes the flaws, inconsistencies, surprises, and limitations that appear along the way.

The Tools Will Improve

As AI generation technology improves, the number of visual mistakes, continuity issues, and unclear story moments will likely decrease over time. Older comics may still show more of those limitations because they reflect the tools and workflow available when they were made.
